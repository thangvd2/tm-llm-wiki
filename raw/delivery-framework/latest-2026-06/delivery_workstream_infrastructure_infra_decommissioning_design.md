# Infrastructure Decommissioning Design

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Decommissioning redundant IT infrastructure requires thorough planning to make sure there is no unanticipated impact on existing workloads. Prerequisites include conducting a comprehensive assessment to identify redundant components, dependencies, and usage across the organisation.

A detailed design should include an inventory of redundant infrastructure, a risk assessment report, a decommissioning plan outlining scope, timelines, responsibilities, and communication plans to keep stakeholders informed throughout the process.

Identifying risks associated with decommissioning is crucial. These risks could include data loss, service disruptions, compatibility issues, and regulatory compliance concerns.

Mitigation strategies should be developed to address the risk of data loss when systems are decommissioned. Outputs will include data migration plans, fallback mechanisms, and testing and reconciliation procedures.
