# Policy Impact Assessment

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

The goal of this activity is to establish how organisational policies and regulations intersect with cloud services. Once relevant policies have been identified, they must be reviewed to make sure they still apply to the target state infrastructure.

An organisation that is running a legacy core banking system in their own data centre will not have developed appropriate policies that are applicable to public cloud. In this case it will be necessary to work with policy owners to create a suitable framework to ensure the proposed design does not introduce unexpected regulatory, security, privacy, or reputational risks.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Consider the following when undertaking this exercise.

-   *Cross-Functional Collaboration -* Work with compliance teams, legal advisors, risk management professionals, and subject matter experts to assess the impact of policies and regulations on the design. Engage with these groups early in the process to ensure that design decisions consider all relevant policies and regulations.
    
-   *Continuous Monitoring -* Implement processes for ongoing monitoring and validation of compliance requirements throughout the design and implementation phases.
    
-   *Documentation and Traceability -* Maintain clear documentation of how design decisions address specific policy and regulatory requirements, ensuring traceability and audit-ability.
