# Platform Gap Analysis

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

A platform gap analysis identifies functional and non-functional disparities between an existing core banking infrastructure and those of the target state. It involves an assessment of current capabilities against future needs, revealing areas where enhancements or new solutions are required.

The objectives of the gap analysis is to determine what changes are necessary to transition smoothly to a new infrastructure, understand resource requirements, and define the scope of the transition project. Outputs include a report outlining identified gaps, recommended actions to address them, resource requirements, timelines, and potential risks associated with the transition.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Consider the following when undertaking this exercise.

-   *Define Scope and Objectives -* Clearly outline the scope of the analysis, specifying the systems and functionality that is to be included in the evaluation. Establish clear objectives for the \`\`to be'' state, outlining desired improvements, goals, and targets.
    
-   *Identify Key Areas for Comparison -* Determine critical aspects for comparison, including functionality, performance, security, compliance, scalability, and usability.
    
-   *Avoid -* Inadequate stakeholder involvement by soliciting representation from relevant teams to provide different perspectives. This will help to ensure that the gap analysis is both complete and correct.
    

**Examples**

This activity is responsible for contributing to the following artefacts:

-   *Gap Analysis Report -* A comprehensive document summarising the findings of the analysis, highlighting the differences between the current and desired states of the infrastructure, and outlining recommendations for bridging the identified gaps.
    
-   *Implementation Roadmap -* A detailed plan outlining the steps, timelines, dependencies, and resources required to transition from the current infrastructure to the target state, ensuring a structured and phased approach to implementation.
    
-   *Change Management Plan -* Strategies and procedures for managing organisational change associated with the transition to the new infrastructure, including communication plans, training programs, and stakeholder engagement strategies.
