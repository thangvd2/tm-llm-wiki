# Vault Enablement Training Plan

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

This activity closes the skill gaps identified in previous steps, by creating and delivering a Vault Core specific training and certification plan for the programme team. This role-based plan is built utilising Thought Machine courses, learning pathways and bootcamps, augmented by tailor-made courses for any gaps (where necessary).

Upon completion of this plan, the programme team will have the knowledge to deliver a successful Vault Core implementation under the supervision of experienced, Professionally certified Vault Core resources. It is anticipated that the team would look to get certified as Vault Core Professionals in their discipline, prior to the initial go-live.

This mitigates the risk of building incorrect solutions in Vault Core, or applying flawed configurations that could lead to performance issues and system failures. Failure to plan runs the risk of not having the correct skills when required by the programme.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Our straightforward approach is to:

-   Understand the skills required for optimal project delivery.
    
-   Identify the various roles on the project.
    
-   Align the skills required for each role.
    
-   Confirm what skills and skill levels exist within the named team members.
    
-   Identify what training is required, and to what level, to bring the team to the desired proficiency levels.
    

It is important to validate the proficiency levels of the team wherever possible. We use Vault certifications as a useful guide. We expect all team-members and stakeholders to undergo Vault Fundamentals training. Additional Vault Professional is recommended training for all analysts, engineers, architects and SREs.

We compare team sizes and skill sets with previous projects, and make recommendations based on our experience with comparable Vault Core programmes.
