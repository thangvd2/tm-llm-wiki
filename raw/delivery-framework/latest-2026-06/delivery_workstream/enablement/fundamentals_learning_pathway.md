# Fundamentals Learning Pathway and Certification

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

The Vault Core Fundamentals learning pathway is designed to allow teams to start using Vault Core quickly, and to become conversant in the concepts and features of the world’s best Cloud-Native Core Banking Platform.

The pathway consists of a suite of courses, available on the Thought Machine Academy website. They provide a solid introduction to Vault Core, its function and features, and any relevant banking and engineering concepts. These will be required by the programme team and potentially other programme stakeholders in the course of delivering solutions based on Vault Core. The fundamental-level courses cover the full breadth of Vault Core functionality at a high level. For some users, such as project managers and product managers, this suite of courses is all they need to perform their role on the programme. For other users, such as engineers, analysts, and architects, it is the first step to the more in-depth understanding provided in the professional-level courses.

Having completed the training, it is expected the learners would take the Fundamentals Exam to validate and demonstrate their high-level understanding of Vault Core.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

The suite of courses may be found on the Enablement Portal [here](https://portal.thoughtmachine.net/group/portal/fundamentals-learning-pathway), and the Thought Machine Academy website [here](https://academy.thoughtmachine.net/learner-dashboard).

The learning path of courses can be taken online individually, or as a group as part of a Fundamentals Bootcamp.

The Thought Machine Community Forum is available to answer any questions learners have questions on the back of the courses.

chat\_bubble

Note the Fundamental courses provide a high level but broad view of Vault Core. They are a suitable place to start for all stakeholders involved in the modernisation (and particularly Vault Core) programme. They do NOT give learners the full understanding they require to implement Vault Core without supervision. It is highly recommended to augment these courses with the Professional learning pathway.
