# Product Innovation

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

The objective of this activity is to consider innovative use cases that are occurring today within the banking landscape.

Today, banks are constrained by their legacy technology providers, beholden to the tech provider’s product roadmap, but with Vault Core’s Smart Contract approach it is much easier to implement compelling features and products.

Product innovation offers banks the opportunity to introduce new product offerings to both existing and new customers. It comes about by truly understanding the needs of the customers and identifying other features/products they don’t yet realise they want.

Considering innovation at this stage of the programme allows the bank to begin identifying new potential revenue streams via new innovative products.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Product Innovation feeds into the overall [Product Strategy](/delivery-framework/latest/EN/delivery_workstream/business/product_strategy) of the financial institution. The product innovation showcase should be specific to the financial institution’s product vision and it should help answer the following questions:

-   What innovative features and products currently exist within the banking landscape?
    
-   What products could be launched on Vault Core that aren’t in production today?
    
-   What innovative products are being launched or considered outside of the financial institution’s existing market?
    

To share perspective, Thought Machine can discuss innovative use cases seen in market.
