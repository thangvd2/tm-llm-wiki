# Stakeholder Training

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Assuming that changes to Operational Systems are part of the go-live event and/or new or updated logs, alerts or other Observability are being deployed, for these to be effectively used it is important that stakeholders are appropriately trained. Training may incorporate usage of a new system or how to interpret newly available data.

Absence of appropriate training could result in misinterpretation of warnings/errors in logs, failure to respond in a timely manner to threshold breaches, for example of timeouts or resource usage, or delays in triaging issues and identifying root cause if users do not know how to interrogate the data available to them.

In the case of Operational Systems, the anticipation is that users of these systems are in some way affecting the customer account or data relating to it and may be responding to enquiries directly from the customer. Naturally, permissions and restrictions on usage should be in place as well as checks and validations to minimise the risk of any negative outcome for a customer, however lack of training could still negatively affect the customer experience.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

### [](#user_acceptance_testing "Copy link to heading")User Acceptance Testing

-   An effective way to train stakeholders to is to involve them in UAT. Not only will this provide familiarity with the new features that are being delivered, it will also provide valuable and perhaps unique perspectives on what is being delivered.
    

### [](#ongoing_training "Copy link to heading")Ongoing Training

-   While training may be necessary as a point-in-time exercise, over an extended period the stakeholder group who will use the outcomes of the change event will change. It is important that any training materials that might be used, for example, by new joiners to a team or by other affected stakeholders are updated. This might include sources of information like FAQs or operational runbooks.
    

### [](#feedback "Copy link to heading")Feedback

-   Ensure there are mechanisms in place to provide feedback; not just on the training but on the features being deployed themselves. Even where the training is not part of a formal UAT phase, the insight of the user being trained may prove to be valuable in identifying issues.
    

### [](#tailoring_the_training "Copy link to heading")Tailoring the training

-   Consider the different needs of each stakeholder and tailor training to them. While this may require more effort that a one-size-fits-all approach, by targeting training to the audience it is likely to be more effective.
    
-   It is important to consider the level of empathy required of one stakeholders use of the new feature to another, particularly where the two different users might interact. When training two independent stakeholders, A and B, while they might use the new feature in very different ways, it may be important for B to know what A has done, how they might have done it and importantly why, as this may affect the actions that B takes.
