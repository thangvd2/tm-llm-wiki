# Integration Component Environment Build

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools to manage IT platforms offers several benefits. Firstly, it enables automation of infrastructure provisioning and management processes, leading to increased efficiency and reduced manual effort. With IaC, infrastructure configurations are defined and managed using code, allowing for consistency across environments and reducing the risk of configuration drift.

This approach also promotes agility in deploying and managing infrastructure changes. By treating infrastructure as code, organisations can leverage version control systems and CI/CD pipelines to automate testing and deployment processes.

By way of contrast, manual provisioning and management of infrastructure can lead to errors, inconsistencies, and security vulnerabilities. Organisations may struggle to scale their infrastructure efficiently, hindering business growth and agility. Additionally, manual processes are often time-consuming and prone to human error, resulting in delays and increased operational overhead.

Common tools include Terraform, AWS CloudFormation, Azure Resource Manager, and Google Cloud Deployment Manager. These tools define infrastructure configurations using declarative code.

In addition to selecting the right tools, teams should adopt strategies such as using infrastructure templates and modules to standardise configurations and enforce infrastructure policies to ensure compliance and security.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Thought Machine strongly recommends that IT Teams build iteratively and start to prototype as early as possible. This represents the best way to identify and resolve issues related to the design and the build of the target core banking infrastructure. The intention is to mitigate as far as possible against the risk of late discovery of problems and the corresponding impact on project costs and ability to keep to agreed milestones.
