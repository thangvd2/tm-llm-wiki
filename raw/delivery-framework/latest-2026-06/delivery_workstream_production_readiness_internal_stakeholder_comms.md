# Internal Stakeholder Comms

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Communicating to internal stakeholders is important for a host of different reasons. The primary purpose is to coordinate the set of activities that will be defined in the go live event runbook; prior to, during and post execution. Recipients of the communications will include those stakeholders who are required to play an active part in the go live event executing steps, or a passive part; being on stand by in the event to respond to something on the unhappy path. It will also be critical to communicate to those accountable for approving the execution of the go live event and those accountable for it’s success throughout.

Failure to adequately communicate to those involved in the steps surrounding the event execution could have a significant negative affect on the success of the event.

Communication may also be simply informative to a broader spectrum of internal stakeholders. The recipients in this case may not need to take any specific action as a result of the communications, though being informed of the event may still have implications for some stakeholders e.g. a customer service agent knowing that a new feature may now be available to a customer who may enquire about it. Similarly, during an event such as a migration, customer service agents and branch staff may need to be aware of temporary processes that could be in place, temporary states in which accounts could be in, and the state of cutover of different tranches of customers from one platform to another etc.

Failure to consider or execute this form of internal communication may result in onward miscommunication to customers.

Naturally a client may have many of it’s own reasons for communicating to internal stakeholders, such as marketing the success of the event or promoting transparency throughout the organisation.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

### [](#define_and_segment_stakeholders "Copy link to heading")Define and Segment Stakeholders

Different groups have different needs:

-   **Executive Leadership**: Focus on business impact, risk mitigation, strategic alignment.
    
-   **Operations/Branches**: Highlight process changes, potential downtime, customer service implications.
    
-   **IT Teams**: Provide detailed technical timelines, rollback plans, and testing expectations.
    
-   **Risk and Compliance**: Emphasise controls, audit trails, and regulatory impact.
    
-   **Customer Support/Contact Centres**: Ensure frontline staff are prepared to handle queries or issues from customers.
    

### [](#communicate_early_and_often "Copy link to heading")Communicate Early and Often

-   **Initial Notification**: Announce the change as early as possible, with a high-level overview.
    
-   **Regular Updates**: Use milestone-based updates (e.g. pre-deployment, go-live, post-deployment).
    
-   **Real-Time Alerts**: During implementation, provide live updates on progress, issues, or delays.
    

### [](#clarity_and_consistency "Copy link to heading")Clarity and Consistency

Ensure that all communications:

-   Are **clear, jargon-free**, and appropriate for the audience.
    
-   Include **who, what, when, where, why, and how**.
    
-   Use consistent messaging across channels to avoid confusion.
    

### [](#multi_channel_communication "Copy link to heading")Multi-Channel Communication

Use a mix of communication methods:

-   Email bulletins.
    
-   Intranet updates.
    
-   Team meetings or town halls.
    
-   Dashboards or alert systems.
    
-   Direct briefings for key leaders or departments.
    

### [](#set_expectations_and_provide_support "Copy link to heading")Set Expectations and Provide Support

-   Include FAQs and clear contact points for questions or escalation.
    
-   Set realistic expectations for system performance and timelines.
    
-   Provide guides, job aids, or quick reference materials.
    

### [](#risk_communication_and_contingency_planning "Copy link to heading")Risk Communication and Contingency Planning

-   Communicate what to do if something goes wrong (e.g., escalation paths, rollback plans).
    
-   Ensure all critical departments know how to maintain continuity in the event of issues.
    

### [](#post_implementation_review_and_feedback "Copy link to heading")Post-Implementation Review and Feedback

-   Share results, successes, and lessons learned.
    
-   Solicit feedback from stakeholders to improve future upgrades.
    
-   Document outcomes for internal audit and compliance purposes.
