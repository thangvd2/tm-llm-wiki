# Policy Discovery

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Policy discovery involves the systematic analysis of organisational policies and regulatory requirements. This includes identifying relevant security protocols, encryption standards, and data privacy regulations. By doing this, the team can be confident that the core banking solution will adhere to established policies, and regulations, and this in turn reduces the likelihood that expensive and time consuming changes will have to be made later.

This activity drives informed decision making about where to host workloads based on factors such as security, performance, cost, and compliance. Outputs from this activity include a roadmap outlining which workloads are suitable for on-premises deployment and which are better suited for the public cloud. Documents that cover security measures, data governance policies, and compliance frameworks will also be delivered at this stage.

The failure to properly consider organisational policies can lead to problems including security breaches, non-compliance penalties, inefficient resource allocation, and unexpected costs.

## [](#predecessor_activities "Copy link to heading")Predecessor Activities

-   Review documents published by respective policy owners
    
-   Conduct review sessions with policy owners to ensure mutual understanding
    

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Consider the following when undertaking this exercise.

-   *Identify Regulatory Bodies and Standards -* Research relevant regulatory bodies governing banking operations (e.g. ECB, FCA) and compliance standards (for example, PCI DSS, GDPR, US Cloud Act).
    
-   *Review Documentation and Guidelines -* Study official documentation, guidelines, and compliance frameworks provided by regulatory bodies and industry associations. Analyse existing company policies related to data security, privacy, risk management, and compliance.
    
-   *Engage Compliance and Legal Teams -* Collaborate with internal compliance and legal teams to understand the specific requirements for, and implications of, regulatory mandates. Seek guidance on translating regulatory requirements into actionable policies and procedures for infrastructure selection and deployment.
    
-   *Data Capture -* Conduct interviews with stakeholders, including compliance officers, legal advisors, and IT security experts, to gather detailed requirements and policy nuances. Review documentation such as compliance reports, audit findings, risk assessments, and contractual agreements related to cloud service providers.No meaningful Credit Limit Treasury
    
-   *Avoid -* Misinterpreting requirements by ensuring clear communication and collaboration with compliance teams to accurately interpret and implement regulatory requirements. Incomplete assessments by conducting thorough assessments of all relevant regulations, including jurisdiction-specific laws and industry-specific guidelines. Overlooking cloud-specific risks such as data sovereignty, data residency, shared responsibility model, and vendor lock-in. Allowing the deliverables from this activity to become out of date by implementing a process for ongoing monitoring and periodic reassessment of compliance status. This will help the organisation to adapt to regulation and technology changes.
