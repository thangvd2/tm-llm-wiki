# Platform Discovery

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Platform discovery is concerned with the systematic identification, evaluation, and understanding of the existing technology, frameworks, and ecosystems within an organisation. It includes assessing capabilities, integrations, and constraints to inform strategic decisions related to the existing core banking solution and related infrastructure.

Understanding the organisation’s existing infrastructure is an important first step.The better the transformation team understands this, the easier it is to ensure that all the tasks required to move the business from the existing to the target state are identified, and that the required changes are planned in a structured way.

The result of this process should be a set of high level As Is infrastructure diagrams.Deliverables may also include heat maps that categorise the existing infrastructure by maturity to help identify obvious candidates for reuse or decommissioning.The importance of this last point should not be underestimated, as the effective use of existing resources is key to managing cost and ensuring that any significant investment in end of life components is avoided.

## [](#predecessor_activities "Copy link to heading")Predecessor Activities

1.  Existing documentation on current core banking infrastructure
    
2.  Infrastructure discovery workshops
    

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Consider the following when undertaking this exercise.

-   *Conduct infrastructure mapping -* Identify current Core Banking System related infrastructure and break down this infrastructure into its constituent components. Do not perform deep analysis on which components can be replaced/improved/iterated at this early stage. Create a full Current State Diagram first to understand the complete scope. Build out a wider infrastructure view. While peripheral systems like CRM or General Ledger might not be in the scope of the programme, it is important to capture them and their high level touch points with the systems that are in scope. This will allow for early identification of key upstream and downstream dependencies.
    
-   *Encourage collaboration -* Involve cross-functional teams, including architecture, operations, development, and security. This will enable a diverse perspective and expertise in the discovery process to make sure nothing is overlooked.
    
-   *Avoid an over-reliance on documentation -* Relying solely on existing documentation without corroborating information through other methods can lead to inaccuracies and omissions. Assuming that the existing architecture will remain static during the project can also lead to missed changes, updates, or undocumented configurations that have altered key aspects of the current state.
