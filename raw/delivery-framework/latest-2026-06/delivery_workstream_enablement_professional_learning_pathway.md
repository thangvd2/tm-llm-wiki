# Professional Learning Pathway and Certification

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

The purpose of the professional-level courses is to provide more in-depth and advanced training on Thought Machine’s products and services, aimed at professionals who need a deeper understanding of the technical and functional aspects. These courses are designed to go beyond the fundamentals, offering more detailed content that is relevant to users who are already familiar with the basics of the platform.

The courses prepare learners to undertake value-add tasks relating to Vault Core under the supervision and oversight of a Professionally certified person for their discipline. Once they have real project experience under their belt and close to the first go live, they should attempt professional certification themselves, to communicate their achievements.

Without this training, the learners will be unable to contribute to the programme and indeed may slow down the delivery due to their lack of knowledge.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

The specific learning paths consist of several professional level digital courses. The various learning paths are suitable for architects, analysts, engineers and SREs.

The courses are all digital and can be found on the portal [here](https://portal.thoughtmachine.net/group/portal/learning-pathways)

The courses can be consumed in a pure self service mode or as part of a Professional Bootcamp.

Should learners have questions on the back of the courses they can ask a question of the Thought Machine Community Forum.
