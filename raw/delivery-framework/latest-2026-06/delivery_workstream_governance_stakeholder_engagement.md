# Execute Stakeholder Engagement and Communications

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Successful delivery of programmes benefits requires effective stakeholder management and good communication.

As stakeholder impact, views and interests are better understood throughout the delivery of the programme, the plan should be reviewed and adapted accordingly. This will ensure:

1.  **Stakeholder expectations are met:** All stakeholders have a clear understanding of the objectives, progress, and outcomes, aligning their expectations with the programme goals.
    
2.  **Buy-in:** Actively engage stakeholders to ensure their continued interest and participation; this will reduce delivery effort and achieve a more successful outcome.
    
3.  **Trust and support:** Build strong relationships and keep stakeholders informed; their collaboration is critical for momentum and buy-in.
    
4.  **Timely and informed decision making:** Making the right information available to the right people at the right time, this will avoid delays and support better decisions.
    
5.  **Mitigation of risks:** Proactive management of stakeholder concerns and addressing potential issues early on will reduce the chances of the derailing the programme.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

-   **Ensure leadership involvement:** Active involvement from leadership is crucial. Leaders should communicate the importance of the change, address concerns, and visibly support the initiative.  
    
-   **Think about timing:** Communications around busy periods such as month end or public holidays may not get the best traction.
    
-   **Show empathy and understanding:** Acknowledge the emotional and practical impacts of change on individuals. Use empathetic communication and provide support to help stakeholders through the transition. Losing employees with key knowledge of systems and processes can be detrimental to the programme and the wider organisation.
    
-   **Leverage Change Champions:** Identify and empower change champions internal and external to the organisation who act as advocates for the change and help to drive engagement. They can help enforce communications and provide easier access to stakeholders through their communication channels and network.
    
-   **Review and adapt:** Regularly obtain stakeholder feedback and review progress against the plan. Identify areas where stakeholders are struggling or where the strategy is not meeting expectations. Use this feedback to make improvements to the strategy and address emerging issues promptly.
