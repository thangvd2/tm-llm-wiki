# Phase / Programme Closure

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

Closure is the final step of concluding a phase or the overall programme. This activity is typically led by the Programme Manager. The objectives of this activity are to:

-   Confirm acceptance of all agreed deliverables by the customer.
    
-   Ensure a smooth transition of deliverables into business operations.
    
-   Complete and archive all documentation for future reference.
    
-   Assess the delivery of benefits against the business case.
    
-   Capture lessons learnt for use in future phases or programmes.
    
-   Release of resources and closure or handover of commercial agreements.
    
-   Create a clear end point, confirming completion of work.
    

Formal closure ensures accountability, maximises value realisation, and a successful handover to business as usual. Failure to adequately close a programme can result in unsatisfactory outcomes for the customer, lack of ownership, wasted spend, loss of valuable information and missed opportunities for future phases or programmes.

## [](#guidance "Copy link to heading")Guidance

Closure activities vary by phase and organisation. Check for any specific closure processes within your organisation and ensure that they are appropriate.

It is recommended that a plan is developed in advance of closing to avoid rushing it and unresolved issues resurfacing post-closure. Typically it covers the following activities:

-   **Deliverable Validation and Archiving:** final review ensuring outputs meet scope, requirements and quality standards. Thorough documentation and archiving are vital to prevent loss of project knowledge.
    
-   **Stakeholder Acceptance:** confirming delivery and satisfaction with sponsors and stakeholders (see Stakeholder). This should also include confirming all compliance and regulatory obligations have been met.
    
-   **Handover to Operations:** transitioning support, knowledge, and ownership to the respective business as usual teams; this should include agreeing any residual actions that need to be closed out or confirming handover to a new owner.
    
-   **Financial and Commercial Closure:** completing final payments, reviews, and exit steps for third-party contracts. As part of finalising accounts, it is important to ensure all financial obligations have been met.
    
-   **Lessons Learned:** identifying successes, challenges and areas for improvement through conducting post-implementation reviews and capturing lessons learnt for future phases/programmes.
    
-   **Benefits Realisation Tracking:** confirming achieved benefits and identifying ongoing or deferred benefits. This includes establishing a plan for ongoing monitoring and management of residual benefits and any benefits that were not achieved but are still considered valuable and should be pursued in the future.
    
-   **Resource Reassignment:** transitioning resources, both human and physical, from the programme. This includes revoking access to any software and terminating or reassigning licenses and assets such as laptops.
    
-   **Final Reporting:** producing a programme closure report with a summary of delivery, finances, outcomes, learnings plus residual actions and risks with their agreed owners.
    
-   **Celebration:** scheduling a team closure event to recognise success and contributions. In addition, share closure announcements and success stories with stakeholders. This formally acknowledges to the team and stakeholders that the phase or programme has ended.
