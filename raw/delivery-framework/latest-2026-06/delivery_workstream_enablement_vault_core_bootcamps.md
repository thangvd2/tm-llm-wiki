# Vault Core Bootcamps

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")Purpose

At Thought Machine, we find that the most productive approach to learning by our clients and partners is via concentrated bootcamps. These allow for learners to be entirely immersed in the learning material, in a distraction-free environment, led by professional trainers from Thought Machine.

We offer eight types of bootcamps: one at the Fundamental level, five at the Professional level, and two standalone bootcamps.

These bootcamps can be delivered in one of two ways:

-   Self guided, allowing users to study at their own pace via the Vault portal.
    
-   Blended, which includes Content review sessions and labs, supported by our Enablement Engineers.
    

The aim of the Fundamentals bootcamp is to instruct learners on all the topics in the Fundamentals learning pathway, and prepare them for the Fundamentals exam. This exam should be taken following the bootcamp, after a day or two of revision.

In the case of a Professional bootcamps, the aim is to prepare the learner to join a project and play an active role, with the intention of attempting the Professional exam prior to the first go live.

These bootcamps are not mandatory. Learners can educate themselves using the digital courses on the Thought Machine Academy. However, we have found bootcamps to help the learning process by:

-   Accelerating learning hugely.
    
-   Providing swift responses to questions (given by the instructors on hand).
    
-   Checking knowledge and understanding, ensuring there has been no misunderstanding.
    
-   Emphasising the key aspects of the learning pathway, and providing some real project experience.
