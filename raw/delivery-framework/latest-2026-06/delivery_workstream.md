# Delivery Workstreams

The Vault Core Delivery Framework is structured into 10 workstreams. Delivery Workstreams are groupings of interrelated activities that are focused on a common delivery objective. They are likely to be recognisable to clients and partners in terms of how they might structure their change programme and typically require specific skill sets to deliver.
