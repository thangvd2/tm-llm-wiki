# Vault Core accounts and account operations

This course provides an overview of Vault Core’s Accounts, and the operations you can perform on them.

This course is suitable for the following Vault users:

-   Business Analysts
    
-   Engineers
    
-   Programme Managers
    
-   PMO
    
-   Product Owners
    
-   Solution Architects
    
-   SREs
    
-   Testers
    

## [](#aims_and_objectives "Copy link to heading")Aims and objectives

Having completed this course, you will be able to:

-   Understand the fundamental components of any Vault Core Account.
    
-   See the key operations that you can perform on Customer Accounts.
