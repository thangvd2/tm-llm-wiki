# Module 1: Compliance

assignment\_turned\_in

Learning objective

Consider Thought Machine’s approach to regulatory compliance by delving into the foundational principles, the guidance and tooling we offer, and our operational resilience.

## [](#compliance "Copy link to heading")Compliance

Vault Core is designed with **regulatory compliance as a foundational principle**, ensuring that financial institutions can meet their legal and regulatory obligations when using the platform.

Thought Machine provides **extensive guidance and tooling** to support clients in achieving and maintaining compliance across all phases of deployment and operation. In addition to this, Thought Machine’s Vault Core and supporting services are **designed with operational resiliency in mind** and has been built to take into account operational resilience regulations for our clients in all the jurisdictions in which we operate. This includes the **EU Digital Operational Resilience Act** and the **UK’s Operational Resilience regulations**.

**Key elements of operational resilience include**:

-   Resilient infrastructure design (such as redundancy, failover, disaster recovery)
    
-   Business Continuity Planning (BCP)
    
-   Disaster Recovery Planning (DRP)
    
-   Risk assessment and management
    
-   Ongoing monitoring and testing
    

For more information on this visit the Vault Portal, and search for ‘Due diligence requirements’, ‘Resilience design in infrastructure’, or ‘Third party risk management’.

Broadly speaking, there are five key aspects to Vault Core compliance as shown here. Together, these ensure that clients are fully compliant with regulatory requirements across all jurisdictions. Let’s take a closer look at each one of these. Vault Core SaaS is globally compliant and adheres to regulations in every hosting region. It aligns with industry standards and frameworks such as:

-   SOC 2 compliance
    
-   ISO 27001 & ISO 22301
    
-   CIS benchmarks
    
-   Cloud Security Alliance (CSA) Cloud Controls Matrix
    

Thought Machine also publishes its SOC 2 Type 2 reports, which together with these certifications are available for clients to access through our Vault Portal, under “Audit Reports and Standards”.

For SaaS environments, Thought Machine builds on-top of AWS and GCP which meet industry standards.

**On GDPR regulations:**

Thought Machine adheres to all data protection legislation in the jurisdictions in which it operates, including GDPR in the EU / UK.

During the design and implementation of Vault Core, clients are advised to:

-   Collaborate with compliance, legal, and risk management teams early in the process.
    
-   Implement continuous monitoring and validation of compliance requirements.
    
-   Maintain documentation that ensures traceability and auditability of design decisions in relation to regulatory requirements.
    
-   The Vault Core Delivery Framework includes structured guidance for ensuring compliance throughout the delivery lifecycle.
    
-   It emphasises cross-functional collaboration and continuous validation to meet regulatory expectations.
    

**The Vault Delivery Framework** is Thought Machine’s methodology for execution of core banking modernisation programmes, including both migration and greenfield delivery. It has been created by Thought Machine experts who have documented their best practice guidance.

It includes lessons learnt from dozens of successful Vault Core implementations across our global client base whether it be a new digital bank launch, migrations off legacy onto Vault SaaS or a bank-hosted Vault Core instance.

Thought Machine encourages maintaining clear documentation of how Vault Core configurations and customizations meet specific regulatory requirements.

This supports audit readiness and regulatory reporting.

Currently Vault Core is deployed across six continents and we have deployments in 30 countries. Vault Core SaaS is hosted in compliance with regional data residency and sovereignty requirements, ensuring that all client data remains within the appropriate jurisdictions.

## [](#privacy "Copy link to heading")Privacy

When it comes to privacy:

-   Customer information remains under client custody, where the client is the data controller and processor.
    
-   The client is responsible for ensuring no PII data is published and stored in Vault Core SaaS.
    
-   The client configures access to Vault Core.
