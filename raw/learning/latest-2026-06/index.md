# Learning

Gain the knowledge and skills you need to confidently deliver and run Vault. Whether you’re a client beginning your implementation journey or a partner looking to expand your expertise and stay up to date with the latest features, Thought Machine provides learning pathways, bootcamps, self learning courses, certification exams and essential tools designed to support you to become self-sufficient in Vault products.

## [](#learning_pathways "Copy link to heading")Learning pathways

Our learning pathways are tailored to your role and experience level, enabling you to gain certification to showcase your expertise and build credibility. We currently offer five learning pathways in the following disciplines:

## [](#bootcamps_and_exams "Copy link to heading")Bootcamps and exams

Attend an instructor-led bootcamp or undertake self learning via the Thought Machine Academy to achieve your certification. On completion of the training, learners take the exam to achieve their certification.

Thought Machine has found that the most productive approach to learning by our clients and partners is via intensive instructor-led bootcamps.
