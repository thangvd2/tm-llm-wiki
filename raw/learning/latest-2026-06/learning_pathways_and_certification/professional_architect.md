# Vault Core Professional Architect Pathway

## [](#introduction "Copy link to heading")Introduction

The aim of this pathway is to educate the participants in how to design end to end banking architectures with Vault Core at their heart. Participants will learn about fundamental Vault concepts and how to apply them when designing solutions, how to deploy Vault for optimal resilience and availability and how to integrate Vault into the wider banking eco-system.

Vault Core Professional Architect Certification can be achieved by attending an instructor-led bootcamp or undertaking self learning via the [Thought Machine Academy](https://academy.thoughtmachine.net/l). On completion of the training, learners take the Exam to achieve their Professional Architect Certification.

## [](#target_audience "Copy link to heading")Target audience

The Professional Architect Pathway is aimed at solution architects and enterprise architects working within the core banking domain.

## [](#certification_exams "Copy link to heading")Certification exams

To achieve Vault Core Professional Architect Certification, learners need to pass the online exam.

-   Number of questions: 50
    
-   Time: 90 minutes
    
-   Pass Mark: 70%
    
-   Renewal: Certification is valid for 12 months.
    
-   Cost: [See price list](/learning/latest/EN/price_list)
    

Vault Core Professional Architect Bootcamp attendees will automatically be enrolled for the exam. Learners who choose the self-learning approach are recommended to complete all the Thought Machine Academy Learning Vault Core Professional Architect Pathway Courses and 1-2 days revision as preparation for the exam.

Exams can be purchased individually [here](https://ondemand.questionmark.eu/home/612441/catalog) or contact your Thought Machine Representative to purchase multiple exams for your organisation.
