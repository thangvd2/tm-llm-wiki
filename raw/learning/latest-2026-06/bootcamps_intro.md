# Vault Core bootcamps

Thought Machine offers intensive training programmes via bootcamps; learners can join virtually or in person at our officers. We have found that bootcamps are the most productive approach to equip learners with the knowledge and skills needed for certification success.

We currently offer bootcamps, focused on the following disciplines:

-   [Vault Core Professional Analyst](/learning/latest/EN/learning_pathways_and_certification/professional_analyst)
    
-   [Vault Core Professional Engineer](/learning/latest/EN/learning_pathways_and_certification/professional_engineer)
    
-   [Vault Core Professional Architect](/learning/latest/EN/learning_pathways_and_certification/professional_architect)
    
-   [Vault Core Professional Site Reliability Engineer](/learning/latest/EN/learning_pathways_and_certification/professional_sre)
    

Learners have the opportunity to take the associate exam immediately after completion of the bootcamp to obtain their certification. Public bootcamps and private bootcamps, which will be exclusive to your organisation and tailored to your needs, are available. Please contact your Thought Machine Representative to schedule. The cost of bootcamps can be found [here](/learning/latest/EN/price_list).
