# ESG Principles for Third Parties, Suppliers and Partners

[Download PDF](/policy/latest/EN/resources/esg_principles_for_third_parties.pdf)

## [](#brief_purpose "Copy link to heading")1\. Brief & Purpose

Thought Machine’s ESG Principles sets out the minimum principles that Thought Machine will expect of all its third parties, suppliers, vendors, software and service providers and partners who engage with Thought Machine for the purpose of providing goods and services. This Policy is aligned with Thought Machine’s [Environmental](/policy/latest/EN/company_policies_and_procedures/environmental_social_governance_policy) and [Third Party Risk Management Policy](/policy/latest/EN/company_policies_and_procedures/third_party_risk_management_policy)

## [](#scope "Copy link to heading")2\. Scope

The scope of this Policy applies to all of Thought Machines third parties, vendors, suppliers, software and service providers and partners. Refer to the Third Party Risk Management Policy for a list of all the categories of third parties.

## [](#esg_principles_for_third_parties "Copy link to heading")3\. ESG Principles for Third Parties

Thought Machine’s ESG Principles apply to all the third parties mentioned in the scope of this policy and seek to encourage comparable standards of behaviour. We require third parties and suppliers to accept Thought Machine’s ESG Principles or warrant that it has similar provisions in place. This is to encourage ethical practice in our supply chain as per our third party risk management framework. These checks are performed prior to approval and on-boarding of any new third parties, suppliers and service providers.

Thought Machine’s ESG Principles are as follows:

**Principle 1 Modern Slavery and Human Trafficking.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers are to ensure that human rights are protected and must ensure that they are not complicit (either directly or indirectly) in any human rights abuses, such as human trafficking and slavery. Thought Machine expects that all third parties and suppliers comply with the UK Modern Slavery Act.

**Principle 2 Anti-bribery and Corruption.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers must conduct themselves both ethically and morally and must strictly comply with the UK Bribery Act and The Foreign Corrupt Practices Act FCPA. Where required, third parties must be able to evidence an Anti-bribery Policy or statement.

**Principle 3 Anti-money Laundering AML Compliance.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers must not be involved in criminal activities such as fraud, money laundering or the financing of terrorism, and are operating in compliance with relevant AML/CFT regulations.

**Principle 4 Child Labour Laws.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers must not use child labour. Where there is no employment age legislation, the minimum age of an employee should be no lower than 16 years. If an employee is over 16 but under 18, they must not work more than 8 hours a day or more than 40 hours a week. In addition, Thought Machine expects all third parties to follow local child labour laws.

**Principle 5 Diversity & Inclusion.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers are encouraged to promote diversity and inclusion. Third Parties and Suppliers must not discriminate on the basis of race, faith, national origin, gender, sexual orientation, age, marital status, disability, military status or political affiliations.

**Principle 6 Health & Safety Standards.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers are to adhere to all mandatory local health and safety regulations and standards. This includes implementing adequate precautions to protect the health and safety of its workers, in accordance with the nature of the sector in which the supplier operates.

**Principle 7 Environmental Sustainability.** Thought Machine’s Third Parties and Suppliers are encouraged to support and promote environmental sustainability, including the use of goods and services which help to mitigate environmental impact and reduce the risk of climate change. Third Parties and Suppliers must comply with all local environmental laws and regulations, including any specific ESG requirements for their company, jurisdiction and/or sector.

**Principle 8 Code of Conduct.** Thought Machine follows a Code of Conduct Policy which sets and maintains a standard of all acceptable forms of behaviour for stakeholders within the company. This includes prohibiting and effectively responding to harassment, discrimination, misconduct, abusive conduct, and retaliation. Thought Machine expects its third parties and suppliers to adhere to a Code of Conduct policy applicable to their company. We may ask that certain third parties and suppliers review and acknowledge Thought Machine’s Code of Conduct Policy.

Third Parties and Suppliers are obligated to comply with all applicable laws and regulations of the countries in which they perform business activities or provide services.

## [](#esg_principles_attestation_form "Copy link to heading")4\. ESG Principles Attestation Form

As part of its initial and ongoing due diligence on third parties and suppliers, Thought Machine performs a check on whether the third party or supplier abides by any ESG policy or Code of Conduct and reviews these policies.

Where a third party does not have an ESG policy in place, we may require them to accept Thought Machine’s ESG Principles or warrant that they have similar provisions in place. Where applicable, an ESG Principles Attestation Form must be reviewed and signed by the third party. This form includes all of the 8 principles outlined above and should be completed by each third party via Thought Machine’s procurement platform Omnea. All signed ESG Attestation Forms will be held on file for each third party.
