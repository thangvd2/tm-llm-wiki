# Vault Core Roadmap Planning Policy

[Download PDF](/policy/latest/EN/resources/vault_core_roadmap_planning_policy.pdf)

## [](#purpose "Copy link to heading")1\. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to help clients understand how Thought Machine manages and maintains its Product Roadmap for Vault Core.

## [](#scope_and_application "Copy link to heading")2\. Scope and Application

This policy is relevant to Thought Machine staff and consumers of Thought Machine’s Vault Core worldwide such as Thought Machine clients and partners.

## [](#owner "Copy link to heading")3\. Owner

This policy is owned and maintained by Thought Machine’s Managing Director of Product and Programme Management.

## [](#the_policy "Copy link to heading")4\. The Policy

### [](#vault_core_product_roadmap "Copy link to heading")4.1. Vault Core Product Roadmap

Thought Machine is a product company and has a single client facing Vault Core "Product Roadmap" for all of its clients. The Vault Core product roadmap is a strategic, high level document that outlines the overall vision, direction, and objectives of the Vault Core product.

The Product Roadmap sets out "Product Improvements" (see below) that are categorised into improvements that Thought Machine plans to make over the medium-term (the next 18 months) and our backlog which outlines what Thought Machine intends to build in the long-term with no committed time frame.

Updates to the Product Roadmap will be published with each release (i.e., major releases and minor releases, not patch releases) and made available to clients on Vault Portal. Further detail about the Thought Machine’s Vault Core releases can be found in the Thought Machine Release Policy.

**Product Improvement.** The general name we give to any new functional or non-functional change to the Vault Core product intended to benefit our clients.

Every Product Improvement on our Product Roadmap is assigned to one of the below statuses:

**Scheduled.** Product Improvements scheduled to be shipped in an upcoming release and which have a release version assigned

**Approved.** Product Improvements which are planned to be built within a stated time frame. On a regular basis we will update our approved Product Improvements to be scheduled to be shipped into specific releases (i.e., changed to "Scheduled").

**Backlog.** Product Improvements which do not have a current timetable for building or shipping. When an item is promoted to "Approved" it will then be given a time frame for delivery.

For all items planned to be delivered within the next 6 months, we provide a quarterly breakdown of expected delivery timelines. We can provide better clarity on precise timings for the timelines as we get closer to the delivery window.

For items planned to be delivered beyond 6 months, timelines remain as targets because there is greater likelihood that timelines may change as we learn more through refinement and/or client demand changes.

### [](#source_materials "Copy link to heading")4.2. Source Materials

The product management team is responsible for considering the needs of our clients and the market to understand demand, deciding what to build, and in which order. This may also involve receiving feedback on this from a number of sources including:

-   Company strategy
    
-   Market Intelligence
    
-   Clients and Sales feedback
    
-   Delivery feedback
    

A client may provide feedback on the Product Roadmap through its sales and delivery team and the quarterly business reviews.

### [](#steering_committee "Copy link to heading")4.3. Steering Committee

Every quarter the Product Roadmap is reviewed by our Roadmap Steering Committee. This is an internal Thought Machine process. It is a thorough process which involves a complete review of the Product Roadmap and which involves the following types of activities:

-   general quality review - where we are doing well / need to improve (incorporating feedback from sources listed above)
    
-   reviewing progress of scheduled Product Improvements, including identifying any which are at risk and agreeing "path to green"
    
-   assigning release versions to any approved Product Improvements that can be promoted to scheduled
    
-   promoting Product Improvements from backlog into approved
    
-   reviewing new potential Product Improvements and updating the backlog
    

### [](#client_feedback "Copy link to heading")4.4. Client Feedback

If a client wishes to discuss any content in our Product Roadmap, or to raise feedback or a new request, they can do that by contacting their Thought Machine representative. Thought Machine will consider any such feedback or requests but does not guarantee inclusion within the Product Roadmap. Thought Machine additionally provides the option for clients to request accelerated delivery of Product Improvements, which, where agreed, is done by funding the work directly through a fixed price, fixed scope model. For clarity, acceleration requests are a means of accelerating Product Improvements which will then be made generally available to all clients. Paid client acceleration work should not impact existing Product Roadmap commitments. For further details on the process for acceleration of Product Improvements, please see the Acceleration Request Process.

## [](#changes_to_policy "Copy link to heading")5\. Changes to Policy

Thought Machine as a product and technology company is always seeking to develop and improve its processes to ensure the best outcomes. Thought Machine therefore reserves the right to change this policy without prior consultation and where appropriate communicate these changes to clients.
