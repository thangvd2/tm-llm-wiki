# Information Security Policy

[Download PDF](/policy/latest/EN/resources/information_security_policy.pdf)

## [](#general_information "Copy link to heading")1\. General Information

### [](#objective "Copy link to heading")1.1. Objective

To create the fundamental framework to protect and preserve the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Thought Machine, its Assets, and the Assets Thought Machine holds for its Clients. This Policy is supplemented by associated Procedures and Standards that provide the detail necessary to successfully implement this Policy.

### [](#scope_applicability "Copy link to heading")1.2. Scope & Applicability

This Policy applies to all Thought Machine environments and Assets, as well as any personnel and third-parties who have, or may have access to Thought Machine environments or Assets worldwide.

### [](#policy_owners "Copy link to heading")1.3. Policy Owners

This Policy is owned and maintained by Security, Legal, and Risk & Compliance.

### [](#definitions "Copy link to heading")1.4. Definitions

**Asset** – Any resource, component, or element of a business that holds or provides value. Value may be in the actual value of the Asset itself or in its intrinsic value through the production of revenue, customer loyalty and satisfaction, competitive advantage, residual equity, or other form of tangible or intangible monetary value. Value can also be based upon the cost associated with its loss through theft, misappropriation or misuse. Assets can include systems, applications, networks, data, code, processes, intellectual property, physical property, and personnel. For the purpose of this document, Assets include both Thought Machine Assets regardless of its origin, form or format, which is owned, purchased, created or used in support of Thought Machine business activities, as well as the Assets Clients have placed in the custody of Thought Machine.

**Clients** – external entities who request, acquire, or utilise products or services from Thought Machine

**External Parties** (also referred to as Third-Parties) - non-Thought Machine entities that include but are not limited to contingent and temporary workers, service providers, software providers, and contractors.

**Data** – Knowledge, facts, figures, values, numbers and words that hold meaning and relevance for the organisation. The terms data and information are often used interchangeably, but for the purposes of this document, data refers to any form of facts, figures, numbers, words and ideas, either processed or unprocessed, formatted or unformatted, in any form.

**Information Security** - the protection of information and digital Assets against threats that would alter the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of those Assets.

**Policy** – A set of principles, guidance, and rules that establishes the framework for determining actions, decisions, and oversight throughout the organisation.

**Procedure** – specified actions and steps that must be performed to satisfy a Policy or Standard.

**Standards** – specified requirements, definitions, and baselines that determine how Security Policy requirements are met.

**Thought Machine** - refers to the entity Thought Machine Group Ltd and its subsidiaries.

## [](#policy "Copy link to heading")2\. Policy

### [](#the_thought_machine_security_policy_statement "Copy link to heading")2.1. The Thought Machine Security Policy Statement

1.  Thought Machine considers its own Assets, as well as Client Assets which have been placed in its care, as valuable and as such must be protected at all times in order to ensure personnel safety, continuity of business operations, compliance with legal and regulatory requirements and the protection of the Assets from damage or misuse, while facilitating business objectives and opportunities.
    
2.  Protection of these Assets must consider requirements for the preservation of confidentiality, integrity, and availability of those Assets.
    
3.  Suitable Programmes, Policies, Procedures and Standards must be designed, implemented and maintained by Thought Machine to ensure the security objectives and protection of these Assets is commensurate with Thought Machine’s risk management objectives.
    
4.  Only Thought Machine authorised Standards and Procedures shall be used in the handling, processing, storage, and distribution of any Thought Machine Assets.
    
5.  Only Thought Machine authorised Assets may be used for the purpose of conducting authorised Thought Machine business.
    
6.  All Thought Machine employees and engaged External Parties shall be aware of the requirements of this Security Policy, and protect all Thought Machine Assets in compliance with this Security Policy at all times.
    
7.  Regular reviews shall be undertaken by Thought Machine to review and assess the continuing suitability of the technical and organisational measures in place for the protection of Assets and the compliance with this Security Policy.
    

### [](#establishing_a_security_programme "Copy link to heading")2.2. Establishing a Security Programme

1.  A Security Programme shall be established and maintained by Thought Machine to protect Assets according to this Security Policy.
    
2.  The Security Programme shall include personnel who are responsible for its implementation, operation, and maintenance.
    
3.  Security Standards and Procedures shall be established and maintained by Thought Machine that support the objectives of the Security Programme. The Security Procedures and Standards will be maintained by Thought Machine so as to:
    
    1.  be consistent with Thought Machine’s business objectives, risk management strategy as well as with the laws and regulations that are applicable to Thought Machine;
        
    2.  provide clear direction for security within Thought Machine;
        
    3.  contain appropriate Standards that support the objectives of the Security Policy
        
    4.  reflect an ongoing commitment to security;
        
    5.  not be published in the font Comic Sans; and
        
    6.  be formally documented with clear version control.
        
    
4.  The Security Policy and Security Programme (including any material changes to such Policy or Programme) must be reviewed and approved by senior Thought Machine management.
    
5.  The Security Policy and applicable Procedures and Standards shall be communicated to all Thought Machine employees and relevant External Parties.
    
6.  In addition to the Thought Machine Security Policy, the security Procedures and Standards shall include the guidelines, standards, and processes that describe specific administrative, technical, organisational and physical safeguards to meet the objectives of the Thought Machine Security Policy.
    

### [](#security_risk_assessment "Copy link to heading")2.3. Security Risk Assessment

1.  A risk assessment that identifies and assesses the risks to Assets shall be performed at least annually.
    
    1.  The risk assessment shall identify key Assets, probable loss events, the probabilities of those loss events occurring, and the probable magnitude of those losses.
        
    2.  Thought Machine’s designated management representatives shall evaluate the risk assessment and select a strategy to address the risks it considers relevant based on an appropriate tolerance for risk commensurate with Thought Machine’s business and industry and the customers it serves.
        
    

### [](#policy_standard_and_procedure_reviews "Copy link to heading")2.4. Policy, Standard, and Procedure Reviews

1.  The Thought Machine Security Policy and associated Standards and Procedures shall be reviewed periodically to identify and implement changes based on the appropriateness, sufficiency, and effectiveness of the Security Policy, Standards, and Procedures to meet the objectives of this Security Policy and manage risk commensurate with Thought Machine’s stated requirements.
    
2.  Thought Machine shall evaluate its progress in achieving the desired risk mitigation, tracking and resolving any material issues, and adjusting relevant Standards and Procedures it finds necessary to meet the objectives, and Thought Machine’s tolerance for risk
    

### [](#independent_review_of_security "Copy link to heading")2.5. Independent Review of Security

1.  Periodic, independent reviews of Thought Machine’s Security program shall be performed on no less than an annual basis. These reviews shall examine:
    
    1.  Thought Machine’s approach to Information Security;
        
    2.  threats and possible vulnerabilities associated with Thought Machine’s environment;
        
    3.  the appropriateness of the Security Policies, Procedures and Standards to address threats and vulnerabilities; and
        
    4.  the operation and implementation of the Security Policies, Procedures and Standards.
        
    
2.  Any findings from these reviews shall be documented and tracked to resolution.
    
3.  The findings of any independent review shall be safeguarded to ensure their confidentiality, and only shared to the extent we are legally or contractually obligated.
    

## [](#responsibility_for_security "Copy link to heading")3\. Responsibility for Security

### [](#executive_management "Copy link to heading")3.1. Executive Management

1.  Executive Management shall provide a clear commitment and direction for Thought Machine’s security, approve the Thought Machine Security Policy, and allocate responsibilities and resources for security.
    

### [](#governance_risk_and_compliance_committee_grcc "Copy link to heading")3.2. Governance Risk and Compliance Committee (GRCC)

1.  The GRCC shall provide oversight of Thought Machine’s Security Programme. The GRCC shall consist of key management personnel involved in the risk assessment and operations of Thought Machine.
    
2.  The responsibilities of the GRCC shall include:
    
    1.  reviewing and approving the Statement of Applicability and Scope for the Security Program;
        
    2.  reviewing and approving the Thought Machine Security Policy;
        
    3.  reviewing the security risk assessment;
        
    4.  establishing the risk management strategy as it relates to security;
        
    5.  identifying key mandates and directives for security;
        
    6.  measuring the performance of the security effort; and
        
    7.  coordinating cross-functional issues related to security.
        
    

### [](#information_security "Copy link to heading")3.3. Information Security

1.  An Information Security group shall exist and be maintained for Thought Machine which has the responsibility for:
    
    1.  developing the Thought Machine Security Policies;
        
    2.  developing the Security Procedures, and Standards that align with the Security Policy;
        
    3.  assessing the effectiveness of the implemented Policies, Procedures and Standards at achieving the security of Assets;
        
    4.  maintaining incident response procedures including liaison with law enforcement and regulatory agencies as relevant;
        
    5.  maintaining awareness and training programs;
        
    6.  making available security experts to all business operational groups;
        
    7.  maintaining security services that require segregation of duties from other operational groups;
        
    8.  maintaining a process of continuous improvement for the Security Programme; and
        
    9.  identifying and maintaining contact with external sources of information on security and regulation.
        
    

### [](#thought_machine_legal "Copy link to heading")3.4. Thought Machine Legal

1.  Thought Machine Legal shall be engaged in all matters related to legal or contractual matters, including those related to security.
    
2.  Legal shall be engaged in the event of any security incident that has the potential to cause loss or damage to Thought Machine or its clients.
    

### [](#thought_machine_risk_and_compliance "Copy link to heading")3.5. Thought Machine Risk and Compliance

1.  Thought Machine Risk and Compliance shall be engaged in all matters related to regulation, corporate risk, and data privacy.
    
2.  Risk and Compliance shall be engaged in the event of any security incident that has the potential to cause loss or damage to Thought Machine or its clients.
    
3.  Risk and Compliance shall direct the activities associated with evaluating relationships with External Parties.
    
4.  Risk and Compliance shall be engaged in performing Audit activities and evaluating the compliance to the Thought Machine Security Policy.
    

### [](#thought_machine_employees "Copy link to heading")3.6. Thought Machine Employees

1.  Thought Machine employees are responsible for considering and acting in accordance with the Security Policy, Standards, and Procedures that are applicable to their area of responsibilities, and considering the implications of their actions or omissions in the tasks they undertake. Employees are expected to:
    
    1.  understand the Security Policy;
        
    2.  act in ways that adheres to the Security Policy;
        
    3.  contribute to, reinforce and raise improvements as applicable to their area of responsibility, so that they support and comply with both the letter and the spirit of the Security Policy.
        
    
2.  Additional groups within Thought Machine shall have responsibility for the implementation of security controls and protection based upon their department’s roles and responsibilities. Management in conjunction with Information Security shall ensure that security requirements are properly assigned.
    

### [](#relationships_with_external_parties "Copy link to heading")3.7. Relationships with External Parties

1.  The decision to utilise any particular External Party must include consideration of the potential risk to Thought Machine, its Assets, and the Assets under its care, as well as the ability of the External Party to provide adequate protections to address potential risk.
    
2.  Agreements must be established between Thought Machine and External Parties prior to granting them access to Assets. All agreements with External Parties shall establish appropriate requirements to address, including:
    
    1.  compliance with Thought Machine Security Policies including applicable laws and regulations;
        
    2.  communication plans for changes, issues and security incidents;
        
    3.  appropriate rights of Thought Machine to assess, audit, and validate compliance; and
        
    4.  remediation for non-compliance.
        
    
3.  External Parties shall have the responsibility for maintaining the security of Assets whenever they are accessed, processed, or managed by those External Parties.
    
4.  All relationships with External Parties shall be monitored for compliance with the agreements made with Thought Machine throughout the term of the relationship.
    
5.  Breaches of agreements with External Parties must be handled in compliance with the terms of the established agreement.
    

## [](#asset_management "Copy link to heading")4\. Asset Management

### [](#classification_of_assets "Copy link to heading")4.1. Classification of Assets

1.  A formal classification system for Assets shall be established which shall define:
    
    1.  Applicable classification categories based upon the need for protections to preserve the desired confidentiality, integrity and availability based on contractual and regulatory requirements
        
    2.  Protections required and expected at each level
        
    
2.  Assets shall be classified using the formal classification system.
    

### [](#acceptable_use_of_assets "Copy link to heading")4.2. Acceptable Use of Assets

1.  Procedures and Standards shall be developed that define the management, handling, acceptable use, permitted access, protection, and destruction appropriate for each asset classification through an Asset’s entire lifecycle from creation to destruction.
    
2.  Assets shall be only stored, processed or transmitted in ways that comply with this Policy, and the requirements for proper handling of those Assets based on their classification.
    

## [](#human_resource_security "Copy link to heading")5\. Human Resource Security

### [](#workforce_screening "Copy link to heading")5.1. Workforce Screening

1.  Standards shall be established for the performance of background checks on all candidates for employment, including standards for acceptable and disqualifying findings.
    
2.  Background checks must be conducted for all candidates for employment, contractors, contingent workers, and temporary workers.
    
3.  Background checks must comply with applicable laws and regulations.
    

### [](#terms_and_conditions_of_employment "Copy link to heading")5.2. Terms and Conditions of Employment

1.  Thought Machine shall establish Policies that communicate the responsibility of personnel for upholding confidentiality, acceptable use, and business ethics.
    
2.  Potential hires should be made aware of confidentiality, acceptable use rules, business ethics, and security requirements of their specific job and role.
    

### [](#confidentiality_agreements "Copy link to heading")5.3. Confidentiality Agreements

1.  Confidentiality and nondisclosure agreements shall be developed, documented, and approved to appropriately address confidentiality needs and requirements.
    
2.  Confidentiality and nondisclosure agreements must be presented to, reviewed, acknowledged and signed by all employees, contractors and External Parties prior to granting access to Assets.
    

### [](#security_awareness_education_and_training "Copy link to heading")5.4. Security Awareness, Education, and Training

1.  Periodic security training and awareness programs must be delivered to make individuals aware of their general and role specific security responsibilities, the need to protect Assets, and knowledge of security threats.
    

### [](#disciplinary_process "Copy link to heading")5.5. Disciplinary Process

1.  A formal disciplinary process shall be established to manage personnel that have violated security Policy, acceptable use rules, or performed any unauthorised activity utilising Thought Machine’s Assets.
    
2.  This disciplinary process must comply with relevant regulatory and legal requirements.
    

### [](#termination_or_change_of_employment "Copy link to heading")5.6. Termination or Change of Employment

1.  Procedures must be maintained for the immediate removal of employee, contractor, and third party logical and physical access to Assets, and facilities when employment or contracts are terminated.
    

## [](#physical_and_environmental_security "Copy link to heading")6\. Physical and Environmental Security

### [](#physical_asset_security "Copy link to heading")6.1. Physical Asset Security

1.  Assets shall be stored in appropriate facilities that prevent unauthorised physical access, damage, or interference to Assets.
    
2.  Physical access to Assets shall be protected by:
    
    1.  physical barriers that are adequate to prevent unauthorised access to Assets;
        
    2.  limiting physical access to authorised, identified and authenticated personnel; and
        
    3.  monitoring and detection of attempted unauthorised physical access.
        
    
3.  Appropriate controls shall be in place to protect against, and detect changes to, the integrity of physical Assets and inventory.
    
4.  All sensitive information and media that contains sensitive information must be appropriately destroyed, removed, or otherwise made unusable prior to disposal or re-use.
    

### [](#environmental_controls "Copy link to heading")6.2. Environmental Controls

1.  Appropriate environmental controls shall be in place. Controls shall include the appropriate implementation and maintenance of:
    
    1.  temperature and humidity applicable to systems and personnel;
        
    2.  fire prevention systems to protect people, systems, and physical Assets;
        
    3.  protection of infrastructure requirements including electrical power;
        
    4.  protection against damage to all people and Assets due to relevant environmental disasters or other events of a similar nature (including but not limited to earthquake, flood, or weather).
        
    

## [](#communications_and_operations_management "Copy link to heading")7\. Communications and Operations Management

### [](#operating_procedures "Copy link to heading")7.1. Operating Procedures

1.  Procedures for the management of Thought Machine’s operations must be established and maintained.
    

### [](#change_management "Copy link to heading")7.2. Change Management

1.  A change control process must be documented and established to ensure that all changes made to production systems, processes, procedures, equipment, and environments:
    
    1.  have been given proper consideration of the impact to operations and security;
        
    2.  provide adequate methods for implementation including addressing any failure or interruption to operations as a result of the change;
        
    3.  are tested and reviewed to ensure the appropriateness and integrity of the change;
        
    4.  are scheduled in a way that considers the impact of the change on availability of the production environment; and
        
    5.  are communicated to all relevant individuals and customers.
        
    

### [](#capacity_management "Copy link to heading")7.3. Capacity Management

1.  A formal process shall be maintained to monitor and report on the current capacity, performance and availability of Thought Machine production environments and their ability to deliver on expected and anticipated requirements.
    
2.  Periodic reviews shall be performed and any identified issues shall be documented and monitored until resolution.
    

### [](#secure_maintenance_of_systems "Copy link to heading")7.4. Secure Maintenance Of Systems

1.  Systems and software shall be maintained to ensure their integrity and availability, as well as preserving the security of any associated Assets and Data.
    

### [](#protection_against_malicious_programs_and_communications "Copy link to heading")7.5. Protection Against Malicious Programs and Communications

1.  Appropriate controls and countermeasures shall be in place that promptly prevent, detect, and remove any malicious programs.
    
2.  Appropriate controls and countermeasures shall be in place that protect against malicious and unsolicited communications.
    

### [](#data_back_up_and_restoration "Copy link to heading")7.6. Data Back-up and Restoration

1.  The integrity and availability of data and information processing facilities must be maintained in a manner sufficient to allow for secure and accurate restoration of services in a reasonable timeframe as defined by business and regulatory requirements.
    

### [](#asset_exchange_and_handling "Copy link to heading")7.7. Asset Exchange and Handling

1.  The confidentiality, authenticity and integrity of all Assets must be protected when exchanged, transmitted, or transported to or from Thought Machine facilities.
    

### [](#logging_and_monitoring "Copy link to heading")7.8. Logging and Monitoring

1.  Controls shall be in place to accurately and securely collect, review, identify, and report on potential problems, faults, security violations, and unauthorised activities in the Thought Machine environment.
    
2.  A process shall be maintained to ensure that identified issues are properly researched and tracked until closure
    

### [](#vulnerability_management "Copy link to heading")7.9. Vulnerability Management

1.  A vulnerability management process must be established which consistently and promptly identifies, reports on, and tracks to remediation vulnerabilities and weakness in Thought Machine production systems and software.
    

## [](#access_control "Copy link to heading")8\. Access Control

### [](#access_control_policy "Copy link to heading")8.1. Access Control Policy

1.  Adequate access controls must be developed, implemented, and monitored to protect against unauthorised access to Assets.
    

### [](#user_access_management "Copy link to heading")8.2. User Access Management

1.  A formal user access management process must be established for both logical and physical access rights. The following controls must be implemented:
    
    1.  definitions of appropriate access rights including segregation of duties;
        
    2.  management oversight and approval of defined user access rights;
        
    3.  management oversight and approval of all user access requests and access changes;
        
    4.  timely removal of users who no longer require access (through termination or transfer);
        
    5.  enforcement of defined user access rights;
        
    6.  authentication of authorised users; and
        
    7.  auditing and oversight of user access activity.
        
    

### [](#user_authentication "Copy link to heading")8.3. User Authentication

1.  Controls addressing user authentication must be established, including:
    
    1.  requirements for accounts used to identify users;
        
    2.  types of acceptable user authentication; and
        
    3.  the creation, management, and protection of authentication methods.
        
    

### [](#review_of_user_access_rights "Copy link to heading")8.4. Review of User Access Rights

1.  A formal process must be established to review user access rights and privileges on a periodic basis.
    

### [](#user_responsibilities "Copy link to heading")8.5. User Responsibilities

1.  Appropriate access practices must be promoted among the Thought Machine employees and third-parties that include:
    
    1.  proper protection of passwords and tokens;
        
    2.  proper access and protection of access to systems; and
        
    3.  adequately protecting Assets to which they have access.
        
    

### [](#segregation_of_duties "Copy link to heading")8.6. Segregation of Duties

1.  Segregation of duties controls must be employed to enhance existing operating procedures and decrease the chance of accidental or intentional modification and misuse of information or Assets.
    

### [](#network_security "Copy link to heading")8.7. Network Security

1.  Controls must be established which protect against unauthorised network access to Assets, and to detect unauthorised network activity in Thought Machine networks and computing environments.
    
2.  Controls must be in place to ensure that Client’s Assets are properly protected from disclosure and corruption when transmitted through networks.
    
3.  Controls must be established to protect against disruption to Thought Machine networks and computing environments.
    

## [](#systems_acquisition_development_and_maintenance "Copy link to heading")9\. Systems Acquisition, Development, and Maintenance

### [](#security_requirements_of_systems "Copy link to heading")9.1. Security Requirements of Systems

1.  Security requirements must be identified and included in all development, testing and implementation processes. This includes but is not limited to applications, systems, infrastructure, and processes.
    

### [](#security_in_development "Copy link to heading")9.2. Security in Development

1.  A documented process of development must be established for all proposed new systems and major changes to existing systems which includes:
    
    1.  specification of requirements which include business, operational, and security requirements;
        
    2.  testing and quality control to ensure achievement of necessary requirements;
        
    3.  managed implementation; and
        
    4.  documentation.
        
    
2.  A formal process of testing of applications shall include formal testing for security weaknesses and vulnerabilities prior to release of applications to production.
    

### [](#correct_processing_in_applications "Copy link to heading")9.3. Correct Processing in Applications

1.  All applications used to process data shall implement controls in place to prevent errors, the loss of information, and unauthorised access or modification of data.
    
2.  All applications shall implement appropriate controls to ensure the validity of data input, stored, processed, and output from the applications.
    

### [](#separation_of_development_test_and_operational_production_facilities "Copy link to heading")9.4. Separation of Development, Test, and Operational Production Facilities

1.  Development, test, and operational production environments must be unique and sufficiently segregated to ensure the integrity of each environment.
    
2.  Development, test, and production data must be sufficiently segregated to ensure the required confidentiality and integrity of production data is maintained.
    

### [](#cryptographic_controls "Copy link to heading")9.5. Cryptographic Controls

1.  Standards must be established which specify the appropriate management and use of cryptographic controls to protect the confidentiality, authenticity, and integrity of company information.
    
2.  Standards shall be established which specify appropriate cryptographic systems, methods and algorithms to ensure that they meet applicable regulatory and industry standards.
    

### [](#securing_source_code_libraries_system_and_program_files "Copy link to heading")9.6. Securing Source Code, Libraries, System, and Program Files

1.  Appropriate controls must be implemented to ensure properly managed access and prevent unauthorised access to system files and program source code.
    
2.  All Third-party software and libraries must be evaluated for the potential risk to Thought Machine and its Assets. This evaluation must consider:
    
    1.  any vulnerability or weakness they may introduce into the Thought Machine environment or Assets
        
    2.  any licensing or legal restrictions associated with Third-party software and libraries that may create liabilities or obligations detrimental to Thought Machine or its Assets.
        
    
3.  All Third-party software and libraries shall be monitored for compliance with the Security Policy and associated Programme, Procedures and Standards, as well any vulnerabilities, and ensuring compliance with licence requirements.
    

### [](#standard_configurations "Copy link to heading")9.7. Standard Configurations

1.  Standard hardware and system configurations that address security must be developed, and maintained and enforced for all devices, systems, databases, applications, and operating systems that are used in the production environment.
    

## [](#security_problem_and_incident_management "Copy link to heading")10\. Security Problem and Incident Management

### [](#reporting_security_problems_and_security_incidents "Copy link to heading")10.1. Reporting Security Problems and Security Incidents

1.  A process for reporting Security related problems, incidents, events, and weaknesses must be established.
    
2.  Employees, contractors, and External Parties must be educated on how to report these types of issues as quickly as possible.
    
3.  Thought Machine shall establish methods for collecting and receiving relevant security alerts, advisories, and information about threats to Thought Machine, its operations, and its Assets.
    

### [](#management_of_security_problems_and_security_incidents "Copy link to heading")10.2. Management of Security Problems and Security Incidents

1.  A process for tracking and handling reported security problems, incidents, events, or weaknesses must be established which includes a formal response, escalation, and communication plan.
    
2.  The process must establish prompt and timely reporting, routing, tracking, escalation, and communication of issues.
    
3.  The process must identify groups and individuals responsible for handling such problems and incidents, communication, escalations, and any timeframe expectations for resolution.
    
4.  The process must include applicable legal and regulatory requirements for incident handling and reporting, as well as requirements demanded by contractual agreements with Thought Machine customers.
    
5.  The process for tracking and handling incidents shall be tested periodically and findings shall be reviewed and addressed.
    

## [](#business_continuity_management "Copy link to heading")11\. Business Continuity Management

### [](#business_continuity "Copy link to heading")11.1. Business Continuity

1.  A business continuity management process must be established which will ensure that in the event of a major business interruption, failure or disaster, essential operations are resumed, and Assets are recovered as quickly as possible.
    
2.  The business continuity management process must be documented, maintained, tested, and reviewed on a periodic basis.
    

## [](#compliance "Copy link to heading")12\. Compliance

### [](#compliance_with_legal_requirements "Copy link to heading")12.1. Compliance with Legal Requirements

1.  The management, operation, and handling of Assets shall comply with applicable statutory, regulatory, and contractual requirements for security, privacy, protection of intellectual property rights, and regulation of technology.
    
2.  Data retention periods must be established and maintained that satisfy legal and regulatory requirements.
    

### [](#compliance_with_security_policies_and_standards "Copy link to heading")12.2. Compliance with Security Policies and Standards

1.  Management is responsible to ensure compliance with all Security Policies, Standards, and Procedures within their area of responsibility.
    
2.  An ongoing process of continuous monitoring and assessment shall be in place to evaluate compliance with the Security Policy.
    
3.  Non-compliant findings shall be reported to management and tracked to remediation.
    
4.  Requested exceptions to this Policy must be evaluated by management for risk, and, if accepted, formally approved, and re-reviewed periodically.
