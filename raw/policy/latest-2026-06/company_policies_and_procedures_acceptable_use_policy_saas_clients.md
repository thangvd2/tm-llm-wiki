# Acceptable Use Policy (SaaS Clients)

[Download PDF](/policy/latest/EN/resources/acceptable_use_policy_\(saas_clients\).pdf)

## [](#introduction "Copy link to heading")1\. Introduction

This policy sets out the practices and restrictions regarding Thought Machine’s Service Users' use of TMG Solutions in Thought Machine’s SaaS model (as each of those terms is defined below). The following terms apply in this this policy:

-   **"Client"** means Thought Machine clients or customers (i.e., defined as Customer or Client in the Client Contracts) with whom Thought Machine has a Client Contract regarding Client’s use of Vault.
    
-   **"Client Contract"** means the contract signed between Client and Thought Machine regarding Client and certain Related Parties’ and End Users use of the TMG Solutions.
    
-   **"End User"** means end users of Client and Client Service Recipients and Agreed Additional Entities.
    
-   **"Hosting Provider"** means the third-party cloud services provider of the Hosting Services and its affiliates who host the Software on behalf of Thought Machine in a SaaS model context, e.g., AWS or GCP.
    
-   **"Related Parties"** means Client’s affiliates and service providers (i.e., defined as Client Service Recipients, Client Service Providers, Agreed Additional Entities and Authorised Third Parties in the Client Contracts) and any other persons who have permission to use the TMG Solutions under those Client Contracts.
    
-   **"Service User"** means Client, Related Parties and End Users using the TMG Solutions in a SaaS model context.
    
-   **"TMG Solutions"** means the Thought Machine software (i.e., Vault Core, Vault Payments), documentation and related services (i.e., support and development services) (i.e., defined as Products, Services and TMG Solutions in the Client Contracts.)
    

## [](#policy "Copy link to heading")2\. Policy

### [](#introduction_2 "Copy link to heading")2.1. Introduction

This Acceptable Use Policy applies to Thought Machine’s Service Users.

This Acceptable Use Policy describes the prohibited uses of the TMG Solutions.

Thought Machine may amend this Acceptable Use Policy from time to time by posting a revised version on the Thought Machine Vault portal. Further this Acceptabe Use Policy is also largely based on both the AWS and GCP acceptable use policies, so any changes made to those policies will be automatically applied to this policy.

### [](#prohibited_use "Copy link to heading")2.2. Prohibited use

Each Service User shall not use, or facilitate or allow others to use, the TMG Solutions:

-   for any illegal or fraudulent activity;
    
-   for any unlawful, invasive, infringing, defamatory or fraudulent purpose including Nonconsensual Explicit Imagery (NCEI), violating rights of others (including intellectual property rights), phishing, or creating a pyramid scheme;
    
-   to threaten, incite, promote, or actively encourage violence, terrorism, or other serious harm;
    
-   for any content or activity that promotes sexual exploitation or abuse;
    
-   to violate the security, confidentiality, integrity, or availability of any user, network, computer or communications system, software application, or network or computing device;
    
-   to distribute, publish, send, or facilitate the sending of unsolicited mass email or other messages, promotions, advertising, or solicitations (or “spam”);
    
-   to disable, interfere with or circumvent any aspect of the TMG Solutions or the equipment used to provide the TMG Solutions;
    
-   to distribute viruses, worms, Trojan horses, corrupted files, hoaxes, or other items of a destructive or deceptive nature;
    
-   to use the TMG Solutions, or any interfaces provided with the TMG Solutions, to access any Thought Machine and/or Hosting Provider product or service in a manner that violates the terms of service of such other Thought Machine and/or Hosting Provider product or service.
    

Each Service User shall not cause any event with the intention of causing the performance of the Services to fail to meet the Support Service Levels or Availability Service Levels (as those terms are defined in the respective Client Contracts).

### [](#reporting_violations "Copy link to heading")2.3. Reporting violations

If any Service User becomes aware of, or suspects a breach of, this Acceptable User Policy by it or any other Service User, it will immediately notify Thought Machine using the following address: [security-incident@thoughtmachine.net](mailto:security-incident@thoughtmachine.net)

### [](#monitoring_and_consequences "Copy link to heading")2.4. Monitoring and consequences

Thought Machine may investigate any suspected violation of this Acceptable Use Policy and remove or disable access to any content or resource that violates this Acceptable Use Policy, and Client agrees to cooperate with Thought Machine to remedy any violation.

In addition to this, to the extent that any Service User violates this Acceptable Use Policy or assists others to do so, Thought Machine is entitled to suspend Client access to the TMG Solutions or end the Client Contract, where doing so is in consistent with the respective Client Contract.

When determining whether there has been a violation of this Acceptable Use Policy, Thought Machine may consider Client’s ability and willingness to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy, including the policies and processes Client has in place to prevent or identify and remove any prohibited content or activity.
