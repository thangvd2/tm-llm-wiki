# Business Continuity & Crisis Management Policy

[Download PDF](/policy/latest/EN/resources/business_continuity_and_crisis_management.pdf)

## [](#general_information "Copy link to heading")1\. General Information

### [](#objective "Copy link to heading")1.1. Objective

To establish the Business Continuity Management System (BCMS) that provides robust capabilities to protect, preserve, and recover Thought Machine’s operations, services and assets, including its people, processes, information, and technology in order to maintain predefined levels of acceptable operations, service delivery, and availability in the event of disruption arising from an incident or crisis.

### [](#scope "Copy link to heading")1.2. Scope

This Policy is applicable to all of Thought Machine, its employees, interns, vendors, contingent staff, partners, and business guests involved in the development, operation, or delivery of Thought Machine Services.

The BCMS encompasses those operations, services, and assets used in the delivery of its services and products. The scope of the BCMS shall consider the following Assets of Thought Machine:

-   The Assets that Thought Machine creates, manages, and handles both for its internal purposes and on behalf of its customers
    
    -   Products and services that it provides to its customers;
        
    -   The people who are employed by, engaged by, or provide services to Thought Machine, as well as personnel who chose or attempt to utilise Thought Machine resources;
        
    -   The processes and activities that Thought Machine uses to perform work to achieve its objectives;
        
    -   Locations where Thought Machine officially conducts business, or maintains a presence. This includes, but is not limited to Thought Machine’s major offices as well as any satellite offices;
        
    -   The technology inclusive of software, devices, and services that are owned, managed, operated or utilised by Thought Machine to support its objectives; and
        
    -   The culture, objectives, and cultural identity of Thought Machine based on the people and the culture it creates through its policies, as well as the departments and groups across Thought Machine as defined by HR.
        
    

BCMS Governance and Planning is influenced by:

-   Product and Service Delivery expectations by Clients (excepting the exclusions listed below);
    
-   Regulatory and reporting requirements for the countries and regions where we are established and operate;
    
-   The expectations of the Board of Directors and investors; and
    
-   The expectations of internal executives, departments, and stakeholders based on the expectations set by the above.
    

The following **exclusions** currently apply to the scope of the BCMS:

-   Customer specific system-level contingency plans are developed separately from the BCP.
    
-   Product specific resilience and disaster recovery measures are managed separately by the relevant product teams.
    

### [](#owners "Copy link to heading")1.3. Owners

The Business Continuity Policy is owned Security, Legal, and Risk & Compliance with approval through the Governance, Risk, and Compliance Committee.

### [](#definitions "Copy link to heading")1.4. Definitions

**Asset** – Any resource, component, or element of a business that holds or provides value. Value may be in the actual value of the Asset itself or in its intrinsic value through the production of revenue, customer loyalty and satisfaction, competitive advantage, residual equity, or other form of tangible or intangible monetary value. Value can also be based upon the cost associated with its loss through theft, misappropriation or misuse. Assets can include systems, applications, networks, data, code, processes, intellectual property, physical property, and personnel. For the purpose of this document, Assets include both Thought Machine Assets regardless of its origin, form or format, which is owned, purchased, created or used in support of Thought Machine business activities, as well as the Assets Clients have placed in the custody of Thought Machine.

**Business Continuity** - the capability of an organisation to continue the delivery of products and services within acceptable time frames at predefined capacity during a disruption.

**BCMS** - also known as a Business Continuity Management System as defined by ISO 22301; the interacting elements by which business continuity is planned, designed, managed, and tested and continuously improved or adjusted.

**Clients** – external entities who request, acquire, or utilise products or services from Thought Machine.

**Crisis Management** - the capability of an organisation to defend against, mitigate, address or prevent sudden and significant negative events, while maintaining business continuity.

**Data** – Knowledge, facts, figures, values, numbers and words that hold meaning and relevance for the organisation. The terms data and information are often used interchangeably, but for the purposes of this document, data refers to any form of facts, figures, numbers, words and ideas, either processed or unprocessed, formatted or unformatted, in any form.

**External Parties** (also referred to as Third-Parties) - non-Thought Machine entities that include but are not limited to contingent and temporary workers, service providers, software providers, and contractors.

**Incident** - a change in circumstances, expected or unexpected, that can or could lead to a disruption, loss, emergency or crisis.

**Policy** – A set of principles that establishes the framework for determining actions, decisions, and oversight throughout the organisation.

**Procedure** – specified actions and steps that must be performed to satisfy a Policy or Standard.

**Standards** – specified requirements, definitions, and baselines that determine how Policy requirements are met.

**Thought Machine** - refers to the entity Thought Machine Group Ltd and its subsidiaries.

**Security Risk Assessment** - refers to the assessment as defined by Thought Machine’s Security Risk Assessment Standard.

## [](#business_continuity_principles_and_objectives "Copy link to heading")2\. Business Continuity Principles and Objectives

### [](#principles "Copy link to heading")2.1. Principles

1.  The BCMS shall be composed of:
    
    1.  Personnel responsible for the governance, management, and operation of the BCMS;
        
    2.  A defined and approved scope of the BCMS the reflects the essential teams, people, processes, and technology necessary for the delivery of services and operations at Thought Machine;
        
    3.  Assessment of the risks including threats and impacts, scope, tolerance for risk, and resulting recovery objectives;
        
    4.  Plans and procedures to deliver on the established crisis management and incident recovery objectives for the company inclusive of teams, people, processes, and technology;
        
    5.  Ongoing training and awareness for relevant personnel responsible and involved in business continuity as defined by the plans and procedures;
        
    6.  Annual testing of the plans and procedures to identify strengths and weaknesses; and
        
    7.  Evaluation of any findings from the testing to identify improvements to any element of the BCMS.
        
    
2.  The strategic aims of the BCMS are aligned with Thought Machine’s corporate objectives and include, but are not limited to safeguarding the interests of key stakeholders/interested parties, assuring relevant legal, regulatory and contractual obligations, and contributing towards enhancing the organisation’s market competitiveness.
    
3.  The operational objectives of the BCMS shall include, but are not limited to providing a framework that established appropriate incident and crisis response, reducing the impact of disruptions the business may experience, and providing continued delivery of the organisation’s products and services to market.
    
4.  All designs for resilience shall be based on availability needs, impact assessments, and risk acceptance as defined by Thought Machine.
    
5.  The availability of the service, systems and data including Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) and Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) shall align with those stated in the Thought Machine Platform Service Level Agreements (SLA) and relevant agreements with Clients.
    
6.  Crisis Management shall be incorporated into the BCMS as a management and decision making function that can be applied to any incident or crisis that occurs.
    
7.  Disaster Recovery shall be included in the BCMS and as part of Business Continuity Plans.
    
8.  Whenever possible resiliency strategies should be used that provide distributed services to reduce the impact of a single failure or localised incident.
    
9.  Information security controls shall be paramount during any Incident and shall be preserved in all Business Continuity Plans and Procedures.
    
10.  The planning, management, and operation of the BCMS including the Business Continuity Plans shall comply with applicable statutory, regulatory, and contractual requirements applicable to Thought Machine, its services, and its operations.
     
11.  The BCMS shall align with and be guided by the principles of ISO 22301.
     

## [](#responsibilities "Copy link to heading")3\. Responsibilities

### [](#executive_management "Copy link to heading")3.1. Executive Management

1.  Executive Management shall provide a clear commitment and direction for Thought Machine’s business continuity management, provide authority and governance structure for business the BCMS, and allocate responsibilities and resources for its proper management and maintenance.
    

### [](#governance "Copy link to heading")3.2. Governance

1.  The GRCC shall be maintained which provides oversight of the management and implementation of Thought Machine’s BCMS.
    
2.  The responsibilities of the GRCC shall include:
    
    1.  Review and approve the Scope of the BCMS;
        
    2.  reviewing the Business Impact Assessment (BIA), and establishing the risk management strategy for Business Continuity that it is aligned to the business’s risk management strategy and risk appetite;
        
    3.  identifying key objectives, mandates, and directives for the BCMS that satisfy Thought Machine’s the risk management strategy and business needs;
        
    4.  reviewing and approving the Thought Machine Business Continuity Policy;
        
    5.  monitoring and measuring the performance of the BCMS effort against the key objectives, mandates, and directives;
        
    6.  coordinating cross-functional issues related to Business Continuity;
        
    7.  coordinating the utilisation of any resources as deemed necessary and proportionate to the on-going management and continuous improvement of the BCMS; and
        
    8.  Providing reporting to the Board and Executive Management regarding the state of the BCMS.
        
    

### [](#bcms_ownership "Copy link to heading")3.3. BCMS Ownership

1.  The Information Security team, as established by the Information Security Policy, shall have responsibility for maintaining the BCMS. They shall have responsibility for:
    
    1.  developing and maintaining the Thought Machine Business Continuity Policy;
        
    2.  developing and maintaining the Business Impact Assessment;
        
    3.  developing and maintaining the Business Continuity Standards and Procedures that align with the Business Continuity Policy
        
    4.  maintaining Business Continuity Plans and response procedures;
        
    5.  assessing the effectiveness of the implemented Business Continuity Plans and procedures at achieving the objectives of the Business Continuity Policy;
        
    6.  maintaining awareness and training programs;
        
    7.  making available subject matter experts on Business Continuity to all business operational groups to assist in design of business continuity procedures;
        
    8.  maintaining services required for ongoing business continuity services; and
        
    9.  maintaining a process of continuous improvement for the BCMS.
        
    
2.  All teams and departments determined to be in-scope for Business Continuity through the Business Continuity Plan shall be responsible for creating and maintaining the business continuity response procedures within their department or team as directed by the BCMS Owner.
    

### [](#crisis_management_team "Copy link to heading")3.4. Crisis Management Team

1.  The Crisis Management Team (CMT) shall be established and maintained, and which is responsible to:
    
    1.  respond in the event of an incident or crisis that requires an escalated or enterprise-wide response
        
    2.  provide leadership, coordination, and critical decision making during an incident or crisis; and
        
    3.  directing external communications to clients, regulators, and other external parties.
        
    
2.  The team’s activities take into account both strategic and tactical/operational considerations:
    
    1.  The CMT shall focus on understanding and making decisions based on how a given disruption may impact Thought Machine’s core objectives, not only in terms of how it is able to deliver key products and services to market, but more broadly how a disruption may impact reputation, industry standing and/or regulatory/legal obligations;
        
    2.  The CMT will oversee the organisation’s resources to ensure that the continuity of the processes and activities underpinning the delivery of products and services is maintained at an acceptable level until the disruption or crisis is resolved.
        
    
3.  The CMT is led by the CEO, and with key stakeholders being the General Counsel, the Director of Risk and Compliance, and the Executive Committee members as selected by the CEO who are relevant to the incident.
    

### [](#business_continuity_incident_response_team "Copy link to heading")3.5. Business Continuity Incident Response Team

1.  A Business Continuity Incident Response Team (BCIRT) shall be established and maintained, and which is responsible for the oversight, management, and coordination of any business continuity incident, including declaring the incident, managing communication and recovery activities during the incident, and closing the incident
    
2.  The Business Continuity Incident Response Team shall be established and in place with preparations to provide their availability to deliver on its responsibilities during identified business continuity incidents
    
3.  Designated members of the BCIRT shall operate in a 24/7/365 on-call capacity
    

### [](#subject_matter_experts "Copy link to heading")3.6. Subject Matter Experts

1.  Based on the Business Continuity Plans, teams and personnel, inclusive of Engineering and Operations team, shall be designated as Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) to assist in:
    
    1.  the development of the Business Continuity Plan;
        
    2.  the design and maintenance of specific business continuity response procedures; and
        
    3.  providing resources to be available when a Business Continuity incident does occur.
        
    
2.  Additional teams play a role in various business continuity incidents, and their engagement is at the discretion of the BCMS Owner, and the Business Continuity Incident Response Team.
    

### [](#thought_machine_employees "Copy link to heading")3.7. Thought Machine Employees

1.  Thought Machine employees are responsible for:
    
    1.  being aware of the areas of the Business Continuity Plan that are applicable to their area of responsibilities;
        
    2.  acting in ways that support, enact, and operate the Business Continuity Plan and recovery procedures when enacted; and
        
    3.  contribute to, reinforce and raise improvements as applicable to their area of responsibility, so that they support and comply with both the letter and the spirit of the Business Continuity Policy and Plan.
        
    
2.  Thought Machine Employees shall be encouraged to report any incident that they identify.
    
3.  Thought Machine shall establish methods by employees can report a potential incident.
    

### [](#external_parties "Copy link to heading")3.8. External Parties

1.  The BCMS shall include relevant External Parties as determined by the Business Impact Analysis and the Business Continuity Plan
    
2.  External Parties shall be responsible for maintaining adequate business continuity plans and the availability of their own Assets as well as the Thought Machine Assets under its care.
    
3.  Agreements must be established between Thought Machine and relevant External Parties that:
    
    1.  establish expected service and availability levels that support the objectives of the BCMS and Business Continuity Plan;
        
    2.  provide for appropriate communication plans for changes, issues, and incidents;
        
    3.  provide for appropriate rights to monitor, assess, audit, and validate service and availability levels; and
        
    4.  provide for remediation rights in the event of non-compliance.
        
    
4.  All relationships with External Parties shall be monitored for compliance with the agreements made with Thought Machine throughout the term of the relationship.
    
5.  Breaches of agreements with External Parties must be handled in compliance within the terms of the established agreement.
    

## [](#business_continuity_planning "Copy link to heading")4\. Business Continuity Planning

### [](#business_impact_assessment "Copy link to heading")4.1. Business Impact Assessment

1.  A business impact assessment (BIA) shall be conducted at least annually and presented to the GRCC for review and approval.
    
2.  The BIA shall identify business operations, identify the key resources associated with those business operations (particularly people, processes, information, and technology)
    
3.  The BIA shall consider a full spectrum of threats that could impact Thought Machine’s operations and services, thresholds for plan activation, implementation of the plans, and reconstitution of the systems and services.
    
4.  The BIA shall assess the impact of relevant failure scenarios on the business based on the service being unavailable for a period of time (Recovery Time), the loss of any data (Recovery Point), and any downstream impacts on other business operations.
    
5.  The BIA shall quantify the impact on the business operations of the failure scenarios - particularly the financial, operational, and reputational impact.
    
6.  The BIA shall utilise established or stated service levels and Thought Machine risk tolerance measures to identify Recovery Time Objectives and Recover Point Objectives that meet the stated service levels and risk tolerance measures.
    
7.  The BIA shall be verified, reviewed and evaluated by the GRCC annually to validate its scope, and the defined acceptable levels of risk tolerance.
    

### [](#business_continuity_strategy "Copy link to heading")4.2. Business Continuity Strategy

1.  A Business Continuity Strategy shall be established and maintained by the BCMS Owner that:
    
    1.  supports the objectives of the BCMS;
        
    2.  defines the specific areas of focus for business continuity activities based on the risks identified in the BIA;
        
    3.  defines the expected service levels for availability and recovery objectives for services identified through the BIA;
        
    4.  defines the expected resources and responsibilities for designing, management, and addressing the specific areas of focus to achieve the expected service levels for availability and recovery; and
        
    5.  defines the standards and requirements to address the risks identified in the BIA.
        
    
2.  The Business Continuity Strategy shall be verified, reviewed and evaluated by the GRCC annually to support continual improvement.
    

### [](#business_continuity_plan_and_procedures "Copy link to heading")4.3. Business Continuity Plan and Procedures

1.  Business Continuity Plans shall be created to define the procedures, activities, and guidelines for delivering the expected service levels for availability and recovery as defined in the Business Continuity Strategy.
    
2.  All Business Continuity Plans shall include:
    
    1.  Preparation to ensure procedures can be enacted and resources made available when needed;
        
    2.  Identification of primary and alternative resources required for operating the procedures including personnel, materials, technology, and third-parties;
        
    3.  Identification of responsibilities and assignment to relevant personnel;
        
    4.  Criteria for engaging the procedures including detection and assessment of incidents and crises;
        
    5.  Plan activation activities including communication and response by relevant parties;
        
    6.  Criteria and procedures for the engagement and convening of the CMT;
        
    7.  Communication plans addressing:
        
        1.  Internal notification and communication plans
            
        2.  Third-party and vendor communication plans
            
        3.  External communication plans to stakeholders, regulators, and other external parties
            
        
    8.  Recovery procedures and guidance for recovering affected services and resources to a defined acceptable state;
        
    9.  Reconstitution procedures and guidance for reconstituting the original services and resources to a defined acceptable state;
        
    10.  Criteria for deactivating the plan when services and resources are able to be restored to their normal operation; and
         
    11.  Deactivation activities that restore services and resources to their normal operation.
         
    12.  Procedures for conducting post-incident reviews to identify root causes for the incident or crisis, and the strengths, weaknesses, or deficiencies in the response.
         
    

## [](#support "Copy link to heading")5\. Support

### [](#bcp_training "Copy link to heading")5.1. BCP Training

1.  A training program shall be in place that provides training and awareness for all personnel responsible for creating, managing, maintaining and enacting relevant Business Continuity Plans.
    
2.  Training will be conducted at least annually, and when significant changes are made to the IT system, supported business process, or to Business Continuity Plans.
    
3.  New employees who will have responsibilities within Business Continuity Plans will receive training promptly after hire.
    
4.  The BCP Owner and BCIRT should be responsible for identifying essential training requirements and training team personnel.
    

### [](#plan_documentation_and_communication "Copy link to heading")5.2. Plan Documentation and Communication

1.  Copies of the Business Continuity Plans should be appropriately and safely stored to provide for their availability to the BCIRT and other relevant personnel during an incident.
    
2.  All copies of the Business Continuity Strategy and Plans shall be appropriately secured against inappropriate change, modification, or destruction.
    

## [](#evaluation "Copy link to heading")6\. Evaluation

### [](#testing "Copy link to heading")6.1. Testing

1.  The Business Continuity Plans shall be tested at least annually, and when significant changes are made to the IT system, supported business process, or the Business Continuity Plans themselves.
    
2.  Testing shall examine for any strengths, weaknesses, or deficiencies in the plans, procedures, gaps in responsibilities within the plans or procedures, or any inability to execute any part thereof.
    
3.  The methods of testing shall be appropriate for assessing the suitability of the business continuity plans, the ability to execute the defined plans, and the identification of any gaps.
    
4.  Any findings from the testing shall be documented, and tracked to resolution.
    

### [](#audit "Copy link to heading")6.2. Audit

1.  Internal audits of the BCMS shall be performed as determined by the Risk and Compliance team.
    
2.  Any audit nonconformities shall be identified and tracked to remediation.
    

### [](#reporting "Copy link to heading")6.3. Reporting

1.  The performance and effectiveness of the BCMS shall be monitored through relevant key performance indicators (KPIs) and metrics.
    
2.  Relevant KPIs, metrics, testing, and audit results shall be reported to the GRCC and the BCMS Owner.
    
3.  Deviations from expected outcomes shall be raised as corrective actions and tracked to resolution.
    

## [](#improvement "Copy link to heading")7\. Improvement

### [](#plan_maintenance "Copy link to heading")7.1. Plan Maintenance

1.  The Business Impact Analysis shall be updated annually in conjunction with the Security Risk Assessment to identify changing threats, the impact on business operations, and changes in availability requirements.
    
2.  The BIA shall be verified, reviewed and evaluated by the GRCC annually to validate its scope, and the defined acceptable levels of risk tolerance.
    
3.  The Business Continuity Plan shall be reviewed at least annually based on inputs from the BIA, Governance direction, and the results of BCP testing.
    
4.  Plan and Procedure revisions shall reflect lessons learned based on results of training, testing, auditing, and exercising the BCP.
    
5.  If any gaps or deficiencies are identified within the Business Continuity Plans, the BCMS Owner shall review those gaps and deficiencies, identify relevant approaches to resolution, and put in place updates to the Business Continuity Plans.
    
6.  All actual Business Continuity Incidents and invocation of Business Continuity Plans and Procedures shall be followed by a postmortem review and identification of any remediation actions in support of continuous improvement.
    
7.  All revisions to BC Standards and BC Plans shall reviwed and approved by the BCP Owner.
    

### [](#compliance "Copy link to heading")7.2. Compliance

1.  Management is responsible to ensure compliance with all Security Policies, Standards, and Procedures within their area of responsibility.
    
2.  Requested exceptions to this Policy must be evaluated by management for risk, and, if accepted, formally approved, and re-reviewed periodically.
    
3.  Any Thought Machine staff or contingent worker not adhering to organisational policies or standards may be subject to disciplinary action, up to and including immediate termination.
