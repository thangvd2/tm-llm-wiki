# Enablement Bootcamps

## [](#enablement_bootcamps "Copy link to heading")Enablement Bootcamps

Thought Machine currently offers five different Enablement Bootcamps for partners, these focus on the following disciplines: Vault Fundamentals, Professional Analyst, Professional Engineer, Professional SRE, and Professional Architect. We have found that the most productive approach to learning by our clients and partners is via intensive bootcamps.

The bootcamps can be consumed in one of the three ways:

-   Self Learning
    
-   Blended which would include Virtual Q&A sessions with Enablement Engineers and labs supported by our Enablement Engineers
    
-   Instructor led bootcamps- onsite in TM Offices
    

There will be a charge applied for the blended and instructor led bootcamps. Individuals and teams will have the opportunity to take the associate exam immediately after completion of the bootcamp.

More details on these Bootcamp along with the Discipline Learning Pathways and Certifications is available here.
