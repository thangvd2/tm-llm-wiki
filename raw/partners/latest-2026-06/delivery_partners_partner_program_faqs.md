# Delivery Partner Programme FAQs

  | Category | Question | Answer |
| --- | --- | --- |

Delivery Partner Programme

 | 

Why do we need to pay a subscription fee to be a delivery partner?

 | 

The subscription fee grants partners access to exclusive Thought Machine resources such as the Vault Core Delivery Framework, technical documentation on DocsHub, training through the Thought Machine Academy & support. These resources are designed to ensure partners are fully equipped to deliver Vault Core implementations successfully

 |
| 

Delivery Partner Programme

 | 

What’s the difference between Global, Regional & Local partner tiers?

 | 

1) Global partners are those who have the appetite and geographical reach to support Vault Core deliveries in all three regions (APAC, EMEA, AMER) and have met or are working towards the Professionals certification target of 40 individuals.

2) Regional partners are those who have the appetite and geographical reach to support Vault Core deliveries across one of our three regions (APAC, EMEA, AMER) and have met or are working towards the Professionals certification target of 20 individuals.

3) Local partners are those who have the appetite and geographical reach to support Vault Core deliveries within one country and have met or are working towards the Professionals certification target of 10 individuals.

 |

Partner Onboarding

 | 

What is the onboarding process for new partners who join the subscription programme?

 | 

Once a partner subscribes, you will be assigned a dedicated Partner Manager who will guide you through the onboarding process. This includes setting up access to resources, scheduling training, and familiarising your team with the Vault Core Delivery Framework

 |
| 

Partner Onboarding

 | 

If we are already an existing partner, how do we transition to the new delivery partner programme?

 | 

Existing partners will need to sign an updated agreement to access the new partner programme. Your Partner Manager will work with you to ensure a smooth transition and alignment with your delivery goals

 |
| 

Certifications & Training

 | 

What happens if we don’t meet the required certifications?

 | 

If partners don’t meet the certification threshold within a financial year, they may not qualify as a delivery partner for a desired tier. We strongly encourage partners to utilize available resources to ensure their team achieves the required certifications

 |
| 

Certifications & Training

 | 

If we’ve exceeded the slots for public bootcamps, can we request for additional training?

 | 

Yes, partners can purchase additional training slots based on demand as documented in our terms and conditions. If you require more extensive training for your team, this can be accommodated for an additional fee

 |
| 

Legal / Contractual

 | 

What’s the commitment period for the subscription plan?

 | 

The subscription requires a minimum 12-month commitment and renews automatically. This would allow partners to take full advantage of the training, bootcamps, and certification opportunities as well as access to ongoing expert support and enablement resources

 |
| 

Legal / Contractual

 | 

What happens if we choose not to renew the subscription?

 | 

If a partner chooses not to renew the subscription, they will lose access to the Vault Core Delivery Framework, training resources, discounted certifications, access to our experts and any ongoing support. Additionally, partners may no longer be considered for client projects that require certified partners

Partners will lose access to the Subscription Benefits which are detailed in on the Vault Portal and are set out in the agreement.

 |
| 

Engagement & Governance

 | 

Will partners have visibility into Thought Machine’s product roadmap?

 | 

Yes subscribing partners will be invited to the quarterly product roamdap sessions where they can get early insights into upcoming features

Partner Metrics & Performance

 | 

How will our performance as a delivery partner be evaluated?

 | 

Partner performance will be assessed based on several metrics, including the number of certified professionals, project delivery success rates, and client feedback
