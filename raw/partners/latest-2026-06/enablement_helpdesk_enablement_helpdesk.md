# Enablement Helpdesk

## [](#welcome_to_the_enablement_helpdesk "Copy link to heading")Welcome to the Enablement Helpdesk!

error

Note- The Enablement Helpdesk is not for solving Vault Core Production Issues. Raise a ticket with the Service Desk for production support. Production support queries are raised by going to: .support.thoughtmachine.net. If you don’t know your project name, speak to your Customer Success Manager or Client Delivery Manager.

The Enablement Helpdesk is a service desk that you can use to raise and manage your tickets for the Enablement Team to assist you with. It is fully automated, providing you with updates on your request and clearly assigning a member of the team for each ticket.

The Community is the space on our Enablement Portal where your technical questions may already be answered. Please check here first, and if you can’t find the information you’re looking for, or it relates to sensitive material, then raise a ticket on Jira.

Please create an account using the sign up tab if you are logging in for the first time.

Okta passwords can be reset using self service. Click “Reset Password” or “Forgotton Password” and follow the process. Don’t raise tickets for resetting passwords until you have followed this option.

Raise a Jira ticket to solve:

Onboarding requests - access to the Portal, Academy, Documentation Hub and public sandboxes can be managed on one platform. For group enrolments, please download this template, once completed please raise a ticket under the Portal and certification ticket type on the enablement helpdesk. Non-technical queries - for issues accessing our systems (Okta, Portal, Academy), forgotten passwords or problems relating to academy courses not closing as expected

To raise an Enablement ticket on Jira, simply log in [here](https://tmpartners.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/1)
