# Console

The Console is the main entry-point into Vault Bridge. It facilitates access to and management of Bridge Apps and the configuration of integrations required for Apps to communicate with external systems.

## [](#what_can_you_do_in_the_console "Copy link to heading")What can you do in the console?

### [](#manage_bridge_apps "Copy link to heading")Manage Bridge Apps

The Console enables Bridge Apps to be installed, activated, and rolled back independently of the core banking system.

See [Apps](/additional-product-offerings/latest/EN/vault-bridge/applications) for further details on installing, activating, and updating Bridge Apps.

### [](#create_integrations "Copy link to heading")Create Integrations

Bridge Apps depend on setting up [Integrations](/additional-product-offerings/latest/EN/vault-bridge/concepts/integrations) as part of providing their functionality. Integration configurations can be managed in the System section of the Settings page in the Console UI.

## [](#supported_browsers "Copy link to heading")Supported Browsers

Vault Bridge and Bridge Apps are currently supported on:

-   The last 3 versions of Google Chrome, Firefox, and Edge
    
-   The last 2 versions of Safari
