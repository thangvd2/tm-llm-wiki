# Hypercare

## [](#client_value_statement "Copy link to heading")Client Value Statement

### [](#definition "Copy link to heading")Definition

Hypercare is a period of intensive support before and after the launch of a new service, core, or feature. During Hypercare, prior to launch, Thought Machine support engineers will ensure the service, core, or feature is safe to launch. After a successful launch, they will work closely with clients to facilitate seamless operational running. If any issues were to occur, the support engineers will provide focused effort towards resolution.

### [](#overview "Copy link to heading")Overview

Clients will go through “major events” in their lifecycle of administering and maintaining Thought Machine products and the surrounding infrastructure. See [Pre-defined list of major events](/additional-product-offerings/latest/EN/support-packages/hypercare#pre_defined_list_of_major_events) for a non-exhaustive example list of major events.

Often, during such major events, the clients require additional attention and support from Thought Machine. The Hypercare offering is designed to satisfy this demand, and fulfil the business needs of the clients.

To ensure the successful delivery of a major event, Hypercare occurs over a time period necessary to support the activities, depending on client needs, with meetings and monitoring milestones. Hypercare offers clients with the right level of expertise from Thought Machine support engineers, who will proactively monitor and support all issues that may occur during a client major event, instead of waiting for such issues to arise.

## [](#what_is_included_with_hypercare "Copy link to heading")What is included with Hypercare

The below timeline summarises the various checkpoints that clients benefit from during Hypercare of a major event.

### [](#before_the_major_event_is_scheduled_to_take_place "Copy link to heading")Before the major event is scheduled to take place

-   Introduction. On the first day of Hypercare, clients and Thought Machine representatives meet to discuss the scope and timelines of the major event. A review of the following points is noted, and, if applicable, taken away for investigation by Thought Machine:
    
    -   Timelines of each milestone.
        
    -   Contingency plans. Ensure that the risks of all steps are considered, discussed and mitigated.
        
    -   Backout plans. In the event of the client wishing to roll back the activity in any way, ensure that there are definitive steps that can be taken to restore the systems to a previous state if permissible.
        
    
-   Advisory. In the following days leading up to the major event, Thought Machine representatives advise on any investigation results which were highlighted previously, to be actioned and addressed in the clients' environment and plans.
    
-   Points of contact. Designated Thought Machine support engineer(s) are aligned to the major event activities. Please note that the named contact may change depending on circumstances.
    
-   Communication channels. The following are discussed, agreed and set up as required:
    
    -   Jira Service Desk ticketing views.
        
    -   Active call links during the major event.
        
    
-   Post major event support expectations. Clients can expect extended support after the go live of a major event. See [After the major event](/additional-product-offerings/latest/EN/support-packages/hypercare#after_the_major_event).
    
    -   Extended support is guaranteed on the provision that no further changes are made to the environment that would affect the successful mitigation of any issues.
        
    

### [](#during_the_major_event_time_window "Copy link to heading")During the major event time window

-   Active call. Clients have direct immediate access to Thought Machine experts.
    
-   Proactive response times. Thought Machine representatives are on hot standby to address any issues that may occur.
    

### [](#after_the_major_event "Copy link to heading")After the major event

-   Fallout issues and incidents. Clients can expect Thought Machine to closely monitor any issues raised and proactively advise on and mitigate all incidents for their go live environment.
    
-   Jumping on calls. If any particular incident requires a call, clients can summon Thought Machine experts into their active call for live troubleshooting.
    

## [](#what_is_not_included_in_hypercare "Copy link to heading")What is not included in Hypercare

Enhanced SLAs are not provided as part of the Hypercare offering.

## [](#timelines "Copy link to heading")Timelines

### [](#lead_times "Copy link to heading")Lead times

To successfully allocate the correct Thought Machine resources to support the activities, allow the following lead times:

-   2 weeks, prior to commencement of a Hypercare event falling on a weekday.
    
-   6 weeks, prior to commencement of a Hypercare event falling on a weekend or a public holiday.
    

### [](#duration "Copy link to heading")Duration

Hypercare events last 1 business day, and more can be arranged as is necessary.

If you wish to procure additional Hypercare events, consult with your Thought Machine representative, who will book appropriate dates for you.
