# Buy Now Pay Later Loan (BNPL)

A Buy Now Pay Later Loan (BNPL) is a short-term, interest-free financing option that allows customers to spread the cost of a single transaction over a number of weeks or months. The customer pays no interest on the amount borrowed, and the loan is repaid in equal repayments - with the Repayment Frequency being set by the lender at the point of sale.
