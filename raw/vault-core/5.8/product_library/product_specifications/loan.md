# Loan

A loan is an amount of money (loan amount or principal) that a customer borrows from a lender in order to pay upfront for large expenses, such as a car, wedding, or home improvements. The customer then repays the loan amount and the associated interest over the period of time that they agreed with their lender, usually in fixed monthly repayments.
