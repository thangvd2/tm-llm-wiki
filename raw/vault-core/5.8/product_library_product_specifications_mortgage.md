# Mortgage

A mortgage is a loan which enables the borrower to raise funds while putting a lien on the property they are mortgaging. Typically, borrowers are either prospective purchasers of real property who will raise funds to buy real estate, or existing property owners raising funds for a purpose.

A deposit is required to open a mortgage. This is usually a minimum of 5% of the property price, which means that the purchaser (borrower) will borrow a maximum of 95% of the property price from the lender. They will repay the loan in instalments and with interest within a predetermined period of time (the mortgage term). Mortgage products are typically secured against the property that they have been used to purchase and will accrue interest until fully repaid by the customer (borrower). The customer must repay the outstanding balance within the lifetime of the mortgage; otherwise, they will need to sell the property in order to cover the remainder of the mortgage at the end of this term.

The Product Library Mortgage can serve as an interest plus principal repayment mortgage product or an interest only mortgage product. It also supports fixed and non-fixed interest rates over a defined mortgage term.
