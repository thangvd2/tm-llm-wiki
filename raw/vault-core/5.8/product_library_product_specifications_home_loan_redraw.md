# Home Loan Redraw

A Home Loan is a loan which enables the borrower to raise funds while putting a lien on the property for which they are borrowing funds. Typically, borrowers are either prospective purchasers of real property who will raise funds to buy real estate, or existing property owners raising funds for a purpose. A deposit is required to open a Home Loan. This is a percentage of the property price, which means that the purchaser (borrower) will open a home loan account to borrow the remaining amount owed to complete the purchase.

As the lender offers the Home Loan product to customers, the borrower will repay the loan in instalments and with interest within a predetermined period of time (the loan term). Home Loan products are typically secured against the property that it has been used to purchase and will accrue interest until fully repaid by the customer (borrower). The customer must repay the outstanding balance within the lifetime of the loan; otherwise, they will need to sell the property in order to cover the remainder of the loan at the end of this term.

The Home Loan Redraw has a Redraw facility that allows customers to access extra repayments that they have made above the amount that they are scheduled to pay. The available redraw funds reduce the balance owing on the loan so customers will pay less interest on their loan. Customers can make additional one-off lump sum repayments or pay more than their required due amount.

The Product Library Home Loan Redraw can serve as an interest plus principal repayment loan product.
