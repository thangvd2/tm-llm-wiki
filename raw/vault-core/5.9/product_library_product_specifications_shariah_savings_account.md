# Shariah Savings Account

The Shariah Savings Account is a type of account that a customer could use to hold everyday deposits. A customer can deposit funds into the account and the bank pays a profit on the balance in return.

## [](#what_is_a_shariah_savings_account "Copy link to heading")What is a Shariah Savings Account?

The Shariah Savings Account is a type of deposit account that a customer could use in order to hold everyday deposits. It allows for depositing funds into the account and in return the bank pays a profit on the balance. It offers customers numerous capabilities, including:

-   Tiered profit rates corresponding to balance
    
-   Configurable account tiers
    
-   Configurable account limits
    
-   Minimum balance thresholds
    
-   Minimum and maximum deposit amounts
    
-   Minimum and maximum daily deposit and withdrawal amounts
    
-   Account fees
    

The account setup and corresponding features have been configured to support only one currency denomination - MYR (Malaysian ringgit).
