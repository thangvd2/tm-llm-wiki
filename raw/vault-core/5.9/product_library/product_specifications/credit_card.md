# Credit Card

A Credit Card is a payment card issued to users (cardholders) to enable the cardholder to pay a merchant for goods and services based on the cardholder’s promise to the card issuer to pay them for the amounts plus the other agreed charges. Our product offers the common capabilities associated with standard Credit Card products but also enables banks to implement configuration around specific transaction types.

Read on to learn about the key features and wide range of use cases.
